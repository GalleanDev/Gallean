A scattered heap of old skeletal remains lies flat and completely still on the ground, seen from above at a low three-quarter angle, with no flesh, clothing or armor left on the bones. Long arm and leg bones with rounded, knobbed joint ends cross one another, broken curved ribs lie among them, and a skeletal hand spreads its thin, jointed finger bones out to one side. Where a skull lies in the heap, it rests on its side, bleached and cracked, with dark, empty eye sockets. The bones are dry and weathered, a dull ivory yellow stained with gray and brown, with deep dark shadows in the gaps between them.
