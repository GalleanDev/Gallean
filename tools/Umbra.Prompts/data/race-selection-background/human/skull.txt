On the right, a green hillside with a few dark trees slopes down to a rocky bank of wet dark stones and a foaming river of white rapids. The sky is heavy gray and blue storm clouds with a bright white opening. Just right of the monument's base, a single old human skull already rests on the dark rocks, its jaw touching the ground. The lower edge of the picture is plain grass, bare earth and the monument's stone base, one continuous natural landscape from edge to edge.
