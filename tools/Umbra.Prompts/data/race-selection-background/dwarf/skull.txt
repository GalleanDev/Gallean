A still, wide mountain landscape in cold daylight, painted as a dark fantasy matte painting. In the center rises a huge dark gray rock spire, a jagged monolith of cracked granite with a carved interlaced knot rune set into its face and thin streaks of ice in its seams. Smaller broken rock fins flank it, and its wide base spreads out into a field of snow and ice, fully visible down to the bottom of the frame. Behind it stretches a vast frozen range: sharp dark blue peaks on the left and right, glaciers and snowfields rolling into the distance, and banks of white mist and drifting snow between them. The sky is deep blue with bright white clouds and a shaft of pale light falling from the upper left. The foreground is packed snow and blue-white ice. Just right of the rock's base, a single old skull already lies half buried in the snow, its jaw touching the ground. The lower edge of the picture is plain snow, ice and the rock's base, one continuous natural landscape from edge to edge.
