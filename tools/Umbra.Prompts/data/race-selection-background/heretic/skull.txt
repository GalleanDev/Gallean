A still, wide hellish landscape, painted as a dark fantasy matte painting. In the center rises a giant curved horn of dark weathered rock, a tall crescent-shaped spire of cracked brown-gray stone leaning to the right, its wide base planted among piles of broken dark boulders that are fully visible down to the bottom of the frame. Around it stand jagged black mountain peaks, and on the left glowing rivers of red lava run across the dark ground. The sky is a burning storm of blood red and black clouds, broken by a bright white glare in the upper middle. On the right, more sharp black crags rise from a valley of red haze, and a foaming river of white rapids flows over dark red stones in the lower right. Just right of the spire's base, a single old skull already rests on the red rocks, its jaw touching the ground. The lower edge of the picture is plain dark red rock, cracked stone and scree, one continuous natural landscape from edge to edge.
