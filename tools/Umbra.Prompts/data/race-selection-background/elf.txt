A still, wide forest landscape in warm golden light, painted as a dark fantasy matte painting. In the center, a tall glowing crystal of translucent pale green stands on a mound of earth, its surface faintly carved with the flowing image of a woman, and it is gripped at its foot by a tangle of dark twisting roots and branches. The mound spreads into a floor of dry brown leaves, moss and roots, fully visible down to the bottom of the frame. On the left, a massive overhanging cliff of mossy brown rock and ancient tree trunks rises into the canopy. Behind the crystal, a hazy forest glade of tall trees and ferns recedes into amber mist, and a bright sun shines through the treetops at the top of the picture, casting soft beams of light. On the right, a steep slope of brown foliage and old trees rises out of the frame. In the lower right lie a skull and a small glowing golden orb among the roots. The lower edge of the picture is leaf litter, earth and roots, one continuous natural landscape from edge to edge.
