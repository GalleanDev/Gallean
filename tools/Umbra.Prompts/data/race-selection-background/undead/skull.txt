A still, wide desolate landscape under a sickly yellow storm sky, painted as a dark fantasy matte painting. In the center stands a tall dark wooden watchtower on four long stilted legs, its boxy black cabin bristling with iron spikes and topped by a narrow spire. The tower's legs stand on a small mound of earth and stones, fully visible down to the bottom of the frame. Around it spreads a dead forest of bare black and gray trees fading into yellow fog, over dry brown and olive grass. On the left, a dark mountain peak rises behind the dead trees. On the right, giant pale ribs arch out of the ground in the distance, and a foaming river of white rapids runs over dark stones in the lower right. The sky is dark gray clouds over a bright pale yellow haze. Just right of the mound, a single old human skull already rests on the dark rocks, its jaw touching the ground. The lower edge of the picture is plain dry grass, bare earth and the mound under the tower, one continuous natural landscape from edge to edge.
