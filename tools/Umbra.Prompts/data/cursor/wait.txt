A sleeping demon imp lies on its belly with its head resting on the ground and its face tilted upward, chin raised, looking up toward the sky as it sleeps with its eyes closed. Its skin is a strong rusty orange-brown, like burnt sienna, with bright orange and amber highlights along every wrinkle and deep red-brown in the folds; it is not dull, gray or chocolate brown, and it has no glossy white shine. One tall pointed ear stands up from its bald wrinkled head. Its face is a cute cartoon imp's, round and chubby-cheeked, its closed eyes two small curved lines and its nose a little button. One thin arm is folded under its head, its cheek resting on the hand like a pillow as it sleeps. Its other arm lies stretched out along the ground in front of its body, the hand resting flat with its small clawed fingers open. On the right, its lean hindquarters, its two thin legs crossed at the ankles, and its long, thin, pointed devil's tail threaded between them. Beside it stands a tall wooden hourglass with copper-studded round caps and pale sand, which slowly turns over, so that for a moment it is seen end-on as a round wooden disc; a small white letter Z floats up above the sleeper and fades. Drawn small and sharp as a mouse cursor on an empty background. Everything else is empty.
