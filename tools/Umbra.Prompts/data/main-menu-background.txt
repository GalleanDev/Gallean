A still, wide landscape at dusk under a heavy storm sky, painted as a dark fantasy matte painting. On the left, a grove of tall, gnarled black trees with thin twisting branches stands against the clouds, their trunks rising from long pale grass where faint blue wisps of light drift near the ground. The ground slopes into a low hill of dry golden grass lit by a patch of warm light. The whole right half of the picture is open sky and land: churning gray storm clouds break around a bright white gap of sunlight in the upper right, and beneath it a misty valley with distant dark hills and a line of far trees fades into haze. A few bare dark trees stand at the far right edge. In the lower right, a rocky bank of wet dark stones drops into a foaming river of white and pale turquoise rapids that runs out of the frame. Everything is natural scenery from edge to edge, one continuous landscape with open sky above the river and clouds all the way to the top of the frame.
