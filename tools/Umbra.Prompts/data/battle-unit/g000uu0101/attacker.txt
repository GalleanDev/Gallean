Three-quarter front view of a gaunt skeleton of dark brown bone. Its rounded skull has deep hollow eye sockets and sits on a thin neck above a narrow ribcage of thin curved bones. The arms and legs are long bare bones, and the hands and feet are pale gray. A wide dark pelvis sits between the ribs and the legs. It holds a long weapon of dark bone, a straight shaft with pale gray bindings and rib-like spurs branching along its length.
