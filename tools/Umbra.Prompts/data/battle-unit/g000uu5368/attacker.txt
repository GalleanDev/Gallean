Three-quarter front view of a huge hunched demon armored in glossy black chitin. Overlapping plates banded with pale gray cover its body, and set low between its shoulders is a wide fanged maw with rows of pale teeth and a thin red line between them. Long ribbed feelers arc out from its shoulders, and two upright wings of dull tan membrane stretch on ribbed black struts with spiked tips. One of its limbs ends in a pale bone-white claw. It stands on thick banded legs with clawed feet, and a long banded tail curls beside it.
