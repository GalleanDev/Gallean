Three-quarter front view of a stooped hooded figure in a deep purple cloak that falls to the ground behind him. The purple hood is pulled low and forward over his head and hangs down in front where a face would be, its opening filled with flat black shadow; nothing shows inside it. A gold crest of two curved horns rises from the brow of the hood. A broad gold collar of oval links rests on his shoulders over a red tunic with small gold buttons and a dark gray robe belted with gold. His olive-brown scaled arms bend forward. Tan cloth wrappings bind his shins down into sturdy dark brown leather boots.
