Three-quarter front view of a woman in a long slate teal gown. A dark red hood covers her head, and long dark brown hair falls past a pale narrow face. A high black collar rises at her throat above dark red and black armor patterned with red scrollwork across the chest and shoulders. Two red sashes hang from the shoulders down the front of the gown. Dark segmented plates run down both arms to armored gloves. A brown belt crosses the gown at the waist, and she holds a long pale stave with a dark grip band at her hip. The gown falls in wide folds to the ground.
