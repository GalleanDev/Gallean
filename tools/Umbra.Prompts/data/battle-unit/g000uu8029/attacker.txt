Three-quarter front view of a griffin with a heavy shaggy brown ruff around its head. Its pale tan face carries a hooked yellow beak and a dark eye, and pale tan tufts sweep back from the crown. Broad wings of dark gray-brown feathers, banded with pale tan along the leading edge, spread from its shoulders. Brown fur covers the body over paler tan stripes at the flank, the forelegs are pale tan and end in dark talons, and a long thin tail with a small tuft at the tip hangs behind.
