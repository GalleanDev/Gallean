Three-quarter front view of a woman in a long black robe. A tall pointed headdress worked in gold-brown embroidery sits over her head, hung with orange tassels at its corners. Long black hair falls beside a pale face above a white collar. Rounded black shoulder plates cap her sleeves. A gold-trimmed sash gathers the robe above a wide skirt covered with branching red lines. A dark cloak hangs behind her shoulders, and a tall dark pole stands upright beside her. One bare pale hand holds a pale blue beaded chain that hangs from an orange and cream grip, and her bare pale feet show below the hem.
