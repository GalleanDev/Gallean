Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking hunched brute with pale gray-tan hide, thick and leathery. Coarse dark bristly hair covers the crown of its head, which sits low between massive sloping shoulders. Its broad back is creased and wrinkled, and long arms reach the ground, ending in huge hands with thick blunt fingers and pale claws. A dark leather strap with a red-brown edge crosses one shoulder, and a wide belt with hanging straps holds a pale hide loincloth. It stands on huge bare feet with broad blunt toes.
