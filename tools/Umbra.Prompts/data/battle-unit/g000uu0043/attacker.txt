Three-quarter front view of a hulking hunched brute with pale gray-tan hide, thick and leathery. Coarse dark bristly hair covers the crown of its head and spreads across the neck and shoulders. A heavy brow sits above small dark eyes, a broad flat nose and a wide mouth of large square teeth. Its massive sloping shoulders carry long arms that reach the ground, ending in huge hands with thick blunt fingers and pale claws. A dark leather strap with a red-brown edge crosses one shoulder, and a wide belt with hanging straps holds a pale hide loincloth. It stands on huge bare feet with broad blunt toes.
