Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge octopus. Its mantle is a rounded sac of mottled brown and tan, wrinkled with fine streaks. The deeply furrowed head turns to show its side below the mantle. Eight thick tentacles spread from the body across the ground, each ringed with darker brown bands and lined with pale suckers along the underside, tapering to thin whip-like tips. Two of them run far longer and thinner than the rest.
