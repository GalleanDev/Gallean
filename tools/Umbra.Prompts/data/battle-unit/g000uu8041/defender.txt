Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a gaunt man in a tall pale cream cylindrical hat, open at its top. The hat rises straight from the back of his head above a high black collar. A close white sleeveless bodice seamed in dark thread covers his back, and his thin gray arms are bare to brass cuffs at the wrists. Dark trousers and a black apron fall from his waist, with a red panel showing at one side, and dark leg wraps laced in pale cord cover his shins above black boots. He carries two broad dark cleavers with pale steel edges.
