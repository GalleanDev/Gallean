Three-quarter front view of a man in long white robes trimmed with gold. A wide pale hood covers his head and falls in streaked gray-white folds onto a shoulder cape with gold edging. A long gray beard spreads over his chest. The robe has wide bell sleeves with gold cuffs, and its skirt falls over a front panel striped in gold and cream, with a band of gold along the hem. A deep red cloak hangs behind him and shows at one shoulder and down his side. Dark brown gloves hold a long white staff banded in dark brown and topped with twisting golden branches. A dark brown shoe shows at the hem.
