Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in long white robes trimmed with gold. A wide pale hood covers the back of his head and falls in streaked gray-white folds onto a shoulder cape with gold edging. The robe has wide bell sleeves with gold cuffs, and from behind it falls to the ground in heavy vertical folds, with a band of gold along the hem and gold edging down the back seam. A deep red cloak shows in a narrow strip at one side. He holds a long white staff banded in dark brown and topped with twisting golden branches.
