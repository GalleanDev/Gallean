Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a centaur in pale gray plate armor on a gray-white horse body. A dark gray helm with a tall dark red crest covers the back of his head. Ridged pauldrons and ridged back plates cover his shoulders, and a small white emblem sits on the plate between them. A brown leather gauntlet grips a long lance that rises past him. A pale tail hangs from a brown band at its root, and the horse's legs are gray-white with pale socks and dark hooves.
