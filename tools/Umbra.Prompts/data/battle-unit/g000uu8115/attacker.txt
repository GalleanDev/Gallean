Three-quarter front view of a centaur in pale gray plate armor on a gray-white horse body. A long white beard spreads below a dark gray helm with a nose guard and a tall dark red crest. Ridged pauldrons cover his shoulders, and a white cloth hangs across his chest. He carries a large dark gray kite shield embossed with a coat of arms, and a brown leather gauntlet grips a long lance. The horse's legs are gray-white with pale socks and dark hooves.
