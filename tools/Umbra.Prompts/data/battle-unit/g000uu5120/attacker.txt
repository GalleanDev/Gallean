Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged dragon with silver-white scales banded with gray. A swept-back crest of long slender blade-like horns rises from its head above an amber eye, a long narrow snout and a jaw of pale teeth. A pale ribbed underside runs down its long neck to its belly. Large bat wings with silver-gray membranes spread from its shoulders, and a row of small pale spines runs along its back. Four heavy legs end in tan claws, and a long tapering banded tail curls out behind it.
