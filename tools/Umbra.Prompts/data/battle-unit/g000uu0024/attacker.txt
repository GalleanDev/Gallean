Three-quarter front view of a towering suit of dark plate armor, hunched forward. Its helm is a smooth ridged casque with a narrow dark slot across the front and no opening below it. Rows of short pale spikes stand along the shoulders, the arms, the hips and the knees. Bands of ribbed plate wrap the chest and the waist, and a banded skirt hangs over heavy thighs. Faint bronze veins run through the dark metal, and it stands on huge splayed feet with broad armored toes. A long straight dark blade rises at its side.
