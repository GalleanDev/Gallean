Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a towering suit of dark plate armor, hunched forward. A smooth ridged casque sits low between the shoulders, and a rounded crest runs from it down the back. Rows of short pale spikes stand along the shoulders, the arms, the hips and the knees. Bands of ribbed plate wrap the back and the waist, and a banded skirt hangs over heavy thighs. Faint bronze veins run through the dark metal, and it stands on huge splayed feet with broad armored toes. A long straight dark blade rises at its side.
