Three-quarter front view of a tall man in a long white robe. A smooth steel helm covers his head, with dark gold bands and a gold ornament across its front. A long gray beard falls from under the helm to his chest. A brown and gold banded mantle sits over his shoulders, and deep blue panels with gold stripes run down the front of the robe from a brown and gold belt. A black and gold checkered border edges the hem, and red-brown shoes show below it. He wears red-brown gauntlets and holds a long gold staff upright.
