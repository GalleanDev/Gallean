Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in a heavy brown leather coat. A green cloth cap covers the back of his head, and a white tuft hangs on a cord. A tan panel worked with pale scrollwork spreads over his shoulders. The coat back is covered with a pale net of lines. A long brown tube is slung across his back, with a bundle of forked wooden shafts standing out of it. Wide sleeves end at broad reddish hands, and tall boots have wide pale gray cuffs. He holds a short axe with a pale steel head on a banded wooden haft.
