Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in a heavy brown leather coat. A green cloth cap with a brown band sits on his head, and a white tuft hangs on a cord. A long pale blond beard and drooping mustache cover his weathered face, which is pale ivory, not red, with small deep-set blue eyes. Dark green cloth runs down his chest and a green pleated kilt hangs below a brown belt. Wide sleeves end at broad reddish hands, and tall boots have wide pale gray cuffs. He holds a short axe with a pale steel head on a banded wooden haft, and a bundle of forked wooden shafts stands behind his shoulder.
