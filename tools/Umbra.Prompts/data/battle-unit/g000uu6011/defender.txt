Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge winged beast standing on two legs, its hide dark olive-green with yellow-green streaks. Two great bat wings spread wide from its shoulders, their outer surfaces dark green and ribbed, edged along the top with a row of hooked silver-gray spurs. Between them the back of its horned head shows, crowned with silver-gray spikes. Rows of pale spurs run down its back and legs. A heavy ribbed tail curls away behind it, its underside yellow-gold, and ends in a pale blade-like tip.
