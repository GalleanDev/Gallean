Three-quarter front view of a huge winged beast standing on two legs, its hide dark olive-green with yellow-green streaks. Two great bat wings spread wide, their pale gray membranes stretched between dark green ribs and edged along the top with a row of hooked silver-gray spurs. Its horned head sits low between heavy shoulders, crowned with silver-gray spikes above a long narrow snout. Rows of pale spurs run down its thick arms and legs, which end in long claws. A heavy tail sweeps out to one side and tapers to a dark point.
