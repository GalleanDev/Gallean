Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a heavy four-legged beast. Its hide is dark brown and black, rough and deeply wrinkled with pale streaks. The neck rises as a ridged dark column, and a mane of coppery-brown quills fans out around it and runs along the shoulders and flanks. Four thick legs end in wide paws with long pale tan claws, and a long banded tail sweeps away behind it, tapering to a blunt tip.
