Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall tree with arms and legs, its body pale gray-white weathered wood. A great crown of orange and dark green leaves covers its head and shoulders and spreads wide above the back. Long limbs of smooth streaked wood hang at its sides, each ending in a knobbly hand with small tan claws. A dark band wraps the middle of the trunk-like body. Thick legs of the same pale wood end in wide splayed roots that grip the ground like feet.
