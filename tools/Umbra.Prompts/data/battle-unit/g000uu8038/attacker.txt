Three-quarter front view of a tall tree with arms and legs, its body pale gray-white weathered wood. A great crown of orange and dark green leaves covers its head and shoulders and hangs low over the torso. Long limbs of smooth streaked wood reach down from the foliage, each ending in a knobbly hand with small tan claws, and a long thin bare branch juts out beyond one of them. A dark band wraps the middle of the trunk-like body. Thick legs of the same pale wood end in wide splayed roots that grip the ground like feet.
