Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a long four-legged skeletal dragon made of bare brown-stained bone. The body runs away and the neck rises at the far end, so the bare skull and its long jaw break the outline above the shoulders. Bony spikes stand along the neck and the spine, and two upright bone spars rise from the shoulders. A long rib cage curves down each flank with dark red flesh between the ribs. Four skeletal legs end in broad feet with long black claws, and a whip-thin tail of linked vertebrae curves away across the ground.
