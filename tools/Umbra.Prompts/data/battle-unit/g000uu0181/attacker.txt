Three-quarter front view of a long four-legged skeletal dragon made of bare brown-stained bone. Its bare skull juts forward at the end of a thick neck, with rows of jagged teeth in a long jaw and empty eye sockets above them. Bony spikes stand along the neck and the spine, and two upright bone spars rise from the shoulders. A long rib cage curves down each flank with dark red flesh between the ribs. Four skeletal legs end in broad feet with long black claws, and a whip-thin tail of linked vertebrae trails behind the body.
