Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a small tree that stands on its own roots. A dense rounded crown of orange and olive-brown autumn leaves fills almost the whole shape and spills down one side in a long leafy limb. Below it a slender trunk of mottled gray-brown bark shows in ridges and knots, with patches of green moss clinging to it. Near the ground the trunk divides into thick roots that splay outward across the earth like feet, their pale wood scarred and worn.
