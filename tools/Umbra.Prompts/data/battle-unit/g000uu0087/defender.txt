Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a rotting man in a dark red mantle. Spiky black hair stands on the back of his head. The dark red mantle hangs square from the shoulders to the calves, streaked with darker vertical folds. Tan plaques marked with dark strokes sit at the shoulders on either side.
