Three-quarter front view of a slender man in a long charcoal gray gown. A smooth pale gray cap with a gold band sits on his head, and a great pile of long white hair covers his head and falls past his shoulder. He is an elf man, his head small beneath the hair, with only a narrow strip of his lean face and one pointed ear showing beside it. A brown wrap crosses his chest above a dark bodice, and a brown leather belt with a gold clasp gathers the gown at the waist. Dark sleeves end in copper-brown cuffs and bare pale hands. A copper stripe runs down the side of the skirt to a wide copper hem banded with a black key pattern, which reaches the ground.
