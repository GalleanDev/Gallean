Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender man in a long charcoal gray gown. Long white hair covers the back of his head and falls in a broad sweep down his back. A gray wrap lies across his shoulders, and a brown leather belt with a gold clasp gathers the gown at the waist. Dark sleeves end in copper-brown cuffs and bare pale hands. The skirt widens below the belt to a wide copper hem banded with a black key pattern, which reaches the ground.
