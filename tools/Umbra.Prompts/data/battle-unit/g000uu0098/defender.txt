Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a standing skeleton in a long cloak. A tall crown of pale bones and small skulls rises above its shoulders, and long stringy gray hair hangs from its underside. Broad pauldrons of clustered bone sit to either side of a wide olive-gold collar. Below the collar a deep red cloak falls to the ground in heavy vertical folds, with an olive-brown band down the center. A long pale staff stands at its side.
