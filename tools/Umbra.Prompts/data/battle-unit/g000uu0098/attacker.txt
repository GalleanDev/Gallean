Three-quarter front view of a standing skeleton in a long cloak. A tall crown of pale bones and small skulls sits on its gray skull, where red light shows in the eye sockets, and long stringy gray hair hangs beside it. Broad pauldrons of clustered bone cap its shoulders above a bare gray ribcage. A deep red cloak hangs from an olive-gold collar and opens to show an olive-gold lining streaked with yellow. Brown wrappings cover its shins above pale bony feet. It holds a long pale staff, and a smooth brown skull rests in its open bony hand.
