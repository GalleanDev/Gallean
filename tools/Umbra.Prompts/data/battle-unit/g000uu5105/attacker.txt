Three-quarter front view of a slender elf in a long pale gray cloak. Pale mint-green hair sweeps back from his fair face above pointed ears, and green markings cross his cheeks. A dark green pleated mantle covers his chest. Rounded brass pauldrons with dark studded rims sit above pale gray armored sleeves and gauntlets. The cloak falls closed in narrow vertical folds to a wide dark green hem band patterned with white dashes, and a pale boot shows below it. He holds a slender gold staff banded with dark rings and a small curled hook.
