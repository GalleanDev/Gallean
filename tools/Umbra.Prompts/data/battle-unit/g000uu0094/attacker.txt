Three-quarter front view of a heavy four-legged beast. Its hide is dark brown and black, rough and deeply wrinkled with pale streaks. A mane of coppery-brown quills fans out around its plated ridged head and runs along the neck and shoulders, covering the head itself. Broad gray plates run down its chest and belly. Four thick legs end in wide paws with long pale tan claws, and a long banded tail tapers away behind it.
