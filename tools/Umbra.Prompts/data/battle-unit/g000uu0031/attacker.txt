Three-quarter front view of a huge giant wrapped in a deep red robe. The robe is finely pleated and crossed by wide brown and gold bands. A smooth gray dome covers the top of his head, and a golden face with pale eyes sits above a long ragged silver beard. White beads sit along the collar. A broad golden band curves across his shoulders and rises into two points above them, each tipped with a white oval and a white tassel on a red cord. Golden segmented gauntlets with red fingers are clasped together. Pale bare legs and feet with blunt toes show below the robe.
