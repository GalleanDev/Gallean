Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge giant wrapped in a deep red robe. The robe is finely pleated and crossed by wide brown and gold bands, falling to the ground and hiding his legs. A smooth gray dome covers the back of his head, and a long pale gray mass falls from it down his back. A broad golden band curves across his shoulders and rises into two points above them, each tipped with a white oval and a white tassel on a red cord. A dark brown curved piece sits on one shoulder beside the band. The pleats of the robe gather at the neck and spread wide at the hem.
