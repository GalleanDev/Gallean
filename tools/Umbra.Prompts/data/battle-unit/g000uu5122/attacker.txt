Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged dragon with a black hide of overlapping ribbed plates. Its broad head is deeply furrowed and carries a pair of thick curved gray-brown horns that sweep forward past the jaw, with a small amber eye above a long snout lined with gold teeth. Large dark gray-brown leathery wings rise from its shoulders, one spread wide and edged with small pale nubs. It stands on four heavy legs that end in pale bone-colored claws, and a long thick tail banded in the same black plates trails behind it, ridged with blunt spines.
