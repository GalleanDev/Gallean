Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged dragon with a black hide of overlapping ribbed plates. Its ridged neck rises from the shoulders in stacked black bands, and the back of its broad head shows a pair of thick curved gray-brown horns curling down beside it. Large dark gray-brown leathery wings spread from its back, edged with small pale nubs and ribbed with darker veins. It stands on four heavy legs that end in pale bone-colored claws. A long thick tail banded in the same black plates trails behind it, ridged with blunt spines.
