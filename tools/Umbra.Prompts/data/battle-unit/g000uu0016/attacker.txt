Three-quarter front view of a slim woman in light armor. Short auburn hair with a straight fringe frames the plain, unpainted face of a young woman, her skin ordinary and matte, her mouth closed and her look steady. Dark brown studded pauldrons sit on her shoulders above a pale yellow scalloped collar, and her body is covered in gray-white armor with pale yellow trim. Pale yellow sleeves run to dark brown vambraces on her forearms, and a wide dark brown belt is wound twice around her waist. Pale yellow leggings reach brown boots with wide cuffs.
