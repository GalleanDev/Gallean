Three-quarter front view of a lean, crouching goblin with tan-brown skin. A large bare domed head sits low on his shoulders, with large backswept pointed ears, orange eyes, a wrinkled snout and a closed, grim mouth. Ridged brown leather armor covers his torso under a rounded tan leather pauldron, and a segmented steel vambrace guards one forearm. A large curved segmented steel plate sits at his hip above a red cloth at his waist. Pale cloth wrappings bind his shins above brown feet, one in a scuffed metal boot. He holds a long spear with a dark wooden shaft, a red grip band and a dark metal head.
