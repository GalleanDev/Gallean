Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean, crouching goblin with tan-brown skin. A large bare domed head sits low on his shoulders, with large backswept pointed ears. Ridged brown leather armor covers his back under a rounded tan leather pauldron, and a segmented steel vambrace guards one forearm. A large curved segmented steel plate sits at his hip above a red cloth at his waist. Pale cloth wrappings bind his shins above brown feet, one in a scuffed metal boot. A long spear with a dark wooden shaft, a red grip band and a dark metal head rests across his shoulder.
