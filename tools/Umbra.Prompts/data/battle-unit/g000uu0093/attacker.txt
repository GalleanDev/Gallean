Three-quarter front view of a lean dragon with a long serpentine body. Its hide is dark brown and black with banded plates along the back, over a pale tan ribbed belly. Olive-green webbed wings with dark ribs fan out behind its shoulders, and a crest of olive-green webbed fins and thin dark spikes frames its narrow ridged head and long snout. Its limbs are heavy and end in long black claws. A long whip-like tail curves up and away, tipped with a fan of olive-green fins.
