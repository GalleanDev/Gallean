Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean dragon with a long serpentine body. Its hide is dark brown and black with banded plates along the back, over a pale tan ribbed belly. The neck rises and the head is turned aside, so a crest of olive-green webbed fins and thin dark spikes stands out around its narrow ridged head and long snout. Olive-green webbed wings with dark ribs spread low behind the shoulders. A long whip-like tail sweeps away and ends in a fan of olive-green fins, and its limbs end in long black claws.
