Three-quarter front view of a man covered head to foot in a shaggy suit of dark brown and olive leaf strips. A leafy hood hides most of his head, with a pale pointed ear out to one side and a narrow face in shadow inside the opening. A plain brown tunic shows through the strips at his waist, above a pale gray band. Leafy wraps cover his legs down to bare brown feet. He holds a long slender brown bow with pale bindings. A brown quiver banded in green, holding arrows with pale green fletching, stands beside him.
