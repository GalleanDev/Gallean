Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man covered head to foot in a shaggy suit of dark brown and olive leaf strips. A leafy hood covers the back of his head, and the strips hang in ragged layers down his back and shoulders. Leafy wraps cover his legs down to bare brown feet. He holds a long slender brown bow with pale bindings, and a shaft with pale green fletching rises past him. A brown quiver banded in green, holding arrows with pale green fletching, stands beside him.
