Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an armored rider on a gray-white winged horse. The rider wears deep blue plate armor with gold and white scrollwork, and a deep blue helm with a swept-back crest covers his head, with long dark hair at its rim. A long dark blade rises past his shoulder. Broad gray and white feathered wings spread from the horse's back to either side. A long gray-white caparison with wide gold borders hangs over the horse's hindquarters in heavy folds, ending in gold fringe above its gray legs and dark hooves.
