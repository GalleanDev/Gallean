Three-quarter front view of an armored rider on a gray-white winged horse. The rider wears deep blue plate armor with gold and white scrollwork, and a deep blue helm with a swept-back crest sits over his long dark hair and the lined, weathered face of a man in his fifties. A long dark blade rises past his shoulder. Broad gray and white feathered wings spread from the horse's back. Blue and gold armor covers the horse's head, where a cluster of long curved ringed tan horns rises, and a blue and gold band crosses its chest. A long gray-white caparison with wide gold borders hangs over the horse to its gray legs and dark hooves.
