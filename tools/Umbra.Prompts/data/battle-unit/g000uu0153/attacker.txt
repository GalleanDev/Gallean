Three-quarter front view of an old man in a long silver-white pleated robe. Long gray hair and a long gray beard hang over his chest, and his lined face is pale between them. Large fluted pauldrons of silver-gray and rust-brown fan out from his shoulders. A rust-brown chest panel crossed by gold bars covers his torso, with two round cream-gold studs above it and a wide dark belt with a silver rim below. A rust-brown pleated cloak hangs behind him, its outside showing at one side and its pale cream lining at the other, and dark brown boots stand under the hem.
