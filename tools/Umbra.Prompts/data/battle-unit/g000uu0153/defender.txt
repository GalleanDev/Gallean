Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an old man in a long silver-white pleated robe. Long gray hair hangs down the back of his head to his shoulders. Large fluted pauldrons of silver-gray and rust-brown fan out from his shoulders. A rust-brown pleated cloak covers him from the shoulders to the ground, its vertical folds parted by a white lace trim and crossed by a gray band near the hem, and the pleats of the robe show beneath it.
