Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a griffin with a black feathered head and body. Spiky black feather tufts stand up from the back of its head. Glossy black feathers cover its neck, back and long raised wings. Tan leather harness straps show at its sides. Its hindquarters are dark brown swirled with tan scrollwork below a broad tan patch, and shaggy cream feathering covers its legs above black claws. A brown tail ends in a broad paddle of dark fur.
