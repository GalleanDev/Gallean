Three-quarter front view of a huge hunched creature of dark purple ribbed hide. Its low head is crowned with pale tan horns curving up and out, and pale curved tusks rise below them beside a dark red mouth and small yellow eyes. Long ribbed tentacle arms of dark red-purple reach out from its shoulders and sides, each banded in segments and ending in a dark point. Heavy plates ridge its chest and belly, dark folded wings sit behind its shoulders, and it stands on thick legs with dark clawed feet. A long segmented tail curls behind it.
