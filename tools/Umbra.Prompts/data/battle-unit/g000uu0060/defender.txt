Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge hunched creature of dark purple ribbed hide. Its horned head turns to the side at the top of the mass, and a pale curved tusk juts past it. Long ribbed tentacle arms of dark red-purple sweep out from its shoulders and sides, each banded in segments and ending in a dark point. Tall folded wings of dark purple with red-brown ribbing rise from its back, heavy plates ridge its spine, a long segmented tail curls down behind it, and it stands on thick legs with dark clawed feet.
