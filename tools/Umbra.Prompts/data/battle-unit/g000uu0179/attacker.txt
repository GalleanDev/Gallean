Three-quarter front view of a tall dark figure in black robes. A black hood sits at the top of the body and its opening is an empty dark hollow. A wide fan of long black feathered strands spreads around the shoulders, and large skeletal wings of pale tan bone rise behind them, built of long rows of bone rungs. Dark gold armor plates cover one arm and hand, pale bone struts show at the hip, and a deep red cloth hangs from the waist over the black robes. A gray steel greave covers one shin above gold-brown clawed feet.
