Three-quarter front view of a robed figure with a bird's head. A dark gray cloth band is wound across the top of the head above pale cream crown feathers, and long gray-black feathers hang at the sides of the head beside a large hooked yellow beak. A pale gray eye shows beside the beak. A dark gray hood and a gray shoulder mantle sit over a dark navy blue robe that falls in heavy folds to the ground. A pale gray cloak hangs at the side. A reddish brown hand grips a wooden staff with a gold cylindrical head. Pale scaly bird feet with dark claws show below the hem.
