Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged wyrm covered in dark purple ridged scales. Several long ribbed necks rise from its shoulders, each ending in a dark purple head crowned with bone-colored horns and flat tan spikes, and one head is lowered to one side with its jaw in view. Rows of tan spines run along its back. A broad olive-gold plated belly banded across the middle shows beneath the chest. Four thick limbs end in long pale claws, and a heavy tail trails behind and ends in flat pale blades.
