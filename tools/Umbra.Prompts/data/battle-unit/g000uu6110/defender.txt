Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged wyrm covered in dark purple ridged scales. Two long ribbed necks rise from its shoulders, each ending in a head crowned with bone-colored horns and flat tan spikes seen from behind. Rows of tan spines run along its back, and a long segmented tail sweeps away to one side and ends in flat pale blades. Thick limbs spread to either side, ending in long pale claws.
