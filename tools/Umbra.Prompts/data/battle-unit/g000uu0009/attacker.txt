Three-quarter front view of an old man in long robes. Long pale blonde hair and a full blonde beard frame his pale weathered face. A gray cloak with gold trim hangs from his shoulders, and gilded plates with embossed bands cover one shoulder and part of the chest. A white and gold striped panel runs down the front of a gray robe with vertical gold stripes, and a small dark plaque in a gold frame sits on it. He wears dark red-brown gloves and dark red-brown shoes.
