Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a squat heavy winged demon with a low-set head. Its brown-bronze hide is thick and creased over a hunched ridged back bound with dark leather straps. Thick curled gray horns wrap around the sides of the head, and a ridged dark crest caps the skull. Large leathery wings spread wide, their dark brown membranes streaked with tan and carrying clawed spikes along the upper edges. Two dark cloth panels hang from the belt to the ground, a long segmented tail curves down between them, and the thick legs end in broad clawed feet.
