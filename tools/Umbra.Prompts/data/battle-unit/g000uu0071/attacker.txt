Three-quarter front view of a squat heavy winged demon with a low-set head. Its brown-bronze hide is thick and creased, ridged across the chest and belly and bound with dark leather straps. Thick curled gray horns wrap around the sides of the head above small dark eyes, heavy jowls and a dark red mouth line, and a ridged dark crest caps the skull. Large leathery wings spread wide, their brown membranes stretched over dark ribs with clawed spikes along the upper edges. A pale ragged loincloth hangs between thick legs that end in broad clawed feet. A dark crossbow hangs low in one clawed hand.
