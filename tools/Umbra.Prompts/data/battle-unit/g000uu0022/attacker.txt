Three-quarter front view of a winged woman in brass armor, her head small and bowed between two falling curtains of pale gray hair. Broad pale gray feathered wings spread from her back, and a large round brown disc stands behind her head. The hair closes over most of the face, leaving a narrow pale strip in shadow where only the brow and the bridge of the nose catch light, and the mouth is closed and still. A brass cuirass shaped to her chest sits under dark brown pauldrons banded with gold, with white puffed sleeves, black and gold bracers and dark gauntlets on her arms. A dark brown belt crosses her waist above a long cream skirt with orange-gold trim that falls to brown boots.
