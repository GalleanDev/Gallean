Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a merman with no legs, his body tapering below the waist into a thick fish tail. A tall dark teal helmet with a fin-like crest and a swept side flange covers the back of his head. The pale blue-white back of his cuirass sits between rounded bronze shoulder plates, with segmented bronze arm plates down both arms. A ragged dark skirt of hanging strips falls to a wide bronze belt, and below it the tail hangs long and ridged in dark brown, tapering to a folded fluke. He holds a long trident with three prongs.
