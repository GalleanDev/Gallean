Three-quarter front view of a merman with no legs, his body tapering below the waist into a thick fish tail. A tall dark teal helmet with a fin-like crest and a swept side flange covers his head. He wears a teal-green cuirass crossed with pale teal bands, with rounded bronze shoulder plates and segmented bronze arm plates down both arms. A ragged dark skirt of hanging strips falls to a wide bronze belt, and below it the tail is banded in cream-white and dark brown and ends in a folded fluke. He holds a long trident with three prongs.
