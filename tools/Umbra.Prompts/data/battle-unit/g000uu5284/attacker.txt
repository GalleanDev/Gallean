Three-quarter front view of a broad figure in dark bronze armor. A mass of white hair sits above a bronze skull face with small green eyes under an ornate gold crown. Long pale curved horns sweep out from armored shoulder plates of bronze and gold. A tall bronze breastplate patterned with small raised blocks covers the chest and tapers to a point. A deep red cloak hangs from the shoulders to the ground over a dark gray inner robe. Bronze gauntlets and bronze greaves show below, and one gauntlet ends in ornate gold knuckles and long pale claws.
