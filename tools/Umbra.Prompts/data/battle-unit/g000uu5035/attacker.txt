Three-quarter front view of a large spider with a swollen glossy black abdomen marbled with vivid red streaks. Eight long legs spread from a low dark body, their thick upper segments deep red with pale pink joints, tapering to thin black points. The front of its body is dark and knobbed, with short red palps and small red eyes. Fine red veining runs forward over the abdomen from its base. The legs are ridged and segmented, and the two front pairs reach out ahead of the body.
