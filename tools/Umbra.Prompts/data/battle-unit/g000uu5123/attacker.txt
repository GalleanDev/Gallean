Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged dragon with a deep green scaled hide. A broad band of cream-yellow plates runs from its chin down the throat to the belly. Its head carries a crest of golden-tan spikes and a mane of golden-tan fronds along the neck, with a yellow eye above a long green snout. Large leathery wings rise from its shoulders, the spread one showing a tan-gold membrane inside green ribbed edges. It stands on four legs banded in darker green and tipped with golden-tan claws, and a long tapering tail curves behind it, tufted with more golden-tan fronds.
