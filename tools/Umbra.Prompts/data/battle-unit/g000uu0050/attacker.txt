Three-quarter front view of a woman in armor riding a gray horse. A small woman's face sits directly under a small, dark rounded steel helm with an engraved band and pale feathered wings, and a white fur ruff covers her chest below it. She wears dark brown armor with gold-edged plates over a blue skirt, and holds two long slender straight blades, each with a green gem. A large pale feathered wing with a smooth gray scalloped upper edge spreads along its flank. A shaggy mass of tangled dark brown strands covers the mount, over a pale cloth skirt with a gold band along its hem. The horse carries its long gray muzzle low, and its legs end in dark hooves.
