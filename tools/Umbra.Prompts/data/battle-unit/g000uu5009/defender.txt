Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean elf in dark leather armor. A bright green spiky crest covers the back of his head and runs down his nape, and pale pointed ears stand out at both sides. Segmented dark brown leather pauldrons sit above sleeves and bracers of the same leather. A dark green quilted tunic falls to his hips under a wide brown leather belt with a gold stud. Dark brown leather leggings and boots cover his legs. He holds a long tan bow with curled tips.
