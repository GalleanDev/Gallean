Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking hunched wolf-beast standing on two legs. Its hide is dark brown and mottled with gray, and thick matted gray-brown fur covers its head and shoulders and hangs down its back in long strands. The head is turned to the side, so a broad muzzle and a pointed ear break the outline at the shoulder. Heavy muscled arms end in huge hands with long blue-gray claws, and its broad feet carry the same claws. Dirty white cloth wrappings are wound around one forearm and around one shin, and a ragged brown cloth is wrapped at the hips.
