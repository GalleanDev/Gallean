Three-quarter front view of a tall gaunt inquisitor standing still in black and white court dress, lit from the front against his own shadow. His head is a tall dark mitre of stiff gray-black cloth, worn low and seen from slightly above, its front band carrying a small tarnished gold emblem; the brim throws the whole face into shadow, so nothing of it shows above the high black collar. Behind his shoulders two ragged fans of long black feathers open outward, and one spray of pale silver feathers rises among them. Over his chest hang white shoulder capes edged with gold, above a doublet banded in black, white and gold. His hips are wrapped in puffed breeches of cream and black panels with gold trim, and below them black hose run into black boots with gold cuffs. In one hand he holds a tall dark staff whose head is a four-pointed black star flanked by small silver wings. A long black cloak hangs from his shoulders to the ground and spreads wide behind him.
