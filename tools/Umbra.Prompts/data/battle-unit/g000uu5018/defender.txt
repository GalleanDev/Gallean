Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean, crouching goblin with dark red skin. Large pointed ears rise from the back of his head. A large dark gray quiver of arrows with orange fletching lies diagonally across his red back on dark straps. White cloth wrappings bind his forearms and his shins, and gray-blue striped wrappings cover his thighs. Dark red boots with pale soles cover his feet. He holds a long knotted wooden bow strung with a pale cord.
