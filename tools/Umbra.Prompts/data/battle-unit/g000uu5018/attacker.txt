Three-quarter front view of a lean, crouching goblin with dark red skin, his head turned in profile toward his bow. His head is small and gaunt: a thin, wedge-shaped face no wider than his neck, tight skin over a ridged brow and hollow cheekbones, narrowing to a pointed snout and chin, with small amber eyes deep in shadow under the brow.
