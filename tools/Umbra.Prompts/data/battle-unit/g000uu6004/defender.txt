Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an enormously fat squat creature whose head sits on top of its bulging body with no neck. Its hide is warm brown and leathery, creased into deep folds, and the broad back is ridged with heavy horizontal rolls. The crown of the head shows as a smooth wide cap, with pointed ears sticking out at each side. Short thick arms end in wide hands of fat blunt fingers with pale nails, and a long thick tail curls out from under the body to a tapering tip.
