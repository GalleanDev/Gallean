Three-quarter front view of a winged figure in pale blue-gray plate armor. A smooth gold helmet covers the head, and a fan of pale feathers rises behind it. The segmented chest plate sits over a dark gray underlayer, and one arm is cased in ornate gold plate while the other stays pale blue-gray. A pale tan cloth apron hangs from the belt across the hips. One leg is cased in ornate gold plate from the knee down and the other in a banded greave, both ending in dark armored boots. Broad dark blue-gray feathered wings spread behind the shoulders. He holds a long ornate gold polearm with a many-pronged head.
