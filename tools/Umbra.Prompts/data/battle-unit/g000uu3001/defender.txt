Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged figure in pale blue-gray plate armor. Broad dark blue-gray feathered wings spread behind the shoulders, and between their roots a fan of pale feathers rises over the back of the head. A pale tan cloth apron hangs from the belt across the hips. One leg is cased in ornate gold plate from the knee down and the other in a banded greave, both ending in dark armored boots. The plated back sits over a dark gray underlayer. He holds a long ornate gold polearm with a many-pronged head.
