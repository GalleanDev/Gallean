Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a centaur, an old man's torso on a dark brown horse body. Long silver-gray hair falls down the back of his head to his shoulders. Dark brown banded armor covers his back and arms above a skirt of layered dark brown plates. A black saddle blanket sits on the horse body, and a black and white streaked caparison hangs over its flanks down to black hooves. A pale gray tail falls behind. He holds a long spear with a broad gold blade.
