Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large gnarled tree. The trunk is dark brown-black bark cut by deep vertical grooves, with a dark knothole partway up, and it splits at the base into thick knotted roots that spread across the ground like legs. Bare gnarled branches extend from the sides of the trunk and end in twig fingers. A broad canopy of dense dark purple-gray foliage droops over the trunk in ragged trailing strands and hangs down behind it.
