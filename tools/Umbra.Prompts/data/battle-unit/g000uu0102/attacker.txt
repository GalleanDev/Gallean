Three-quarter front view of a large gnarled tree with a face in its trunk. A narrow bright green eye sits above a wide jagged mouth of bright green teeth. The trunk is dark brown-black bark cut by deep vertical grooves, and it splits at the base into thick knotted roots that spread across the ground like legs. Bare gnarled branches extend from the sides of the trunk and end in twig fingers. A broad canopy of dense dark purple-gray foliage droops over the trunk in ragged trailing strands.
