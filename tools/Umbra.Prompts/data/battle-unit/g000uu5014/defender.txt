Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a massive green-skinned orc in heavy armor. A spiked gold crown sits above black hair at the back of his head. A ragged dark brown cloak of hanging strips covers his back almost to the ground, parted by a rounded bronze shoulder guard set with pale spikes and by ornate bronze armbands on his green arms. Steel ankle cuffs sit above green clawed feet below the cloak. A spiked iron ball on a chain hangs from one hand, and a tall banner pole with a gray skull at the top and ragged red and dark green strips rises behind him.
