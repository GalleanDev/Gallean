Three-quarter front view of a massive green-skinned orc in heavy armor. A spiked gold crown sits on his black hair above a heavy green face with yellow eyes and jutting tusks, and a long black beard hangs to his chest. He wears a dark bronze breastplate over overlapping bronze bands across his belly, a rounded bronze shoulder guard set with pale spikes, and ornate bronze armbands on his green arms. A ragged dark brown cloak of hanging strips falls behind him over brown armored leggings, and steel ankle cuffs sit above green clawed feet. A spiked iron ball on a chain hangs from one hand, and a tall banner pole with a gray skull at the top and ragged red and dark green strips rises behind his shoulder.
