Three-quarter front view of a huge squat demon with a bulky purple body of knotted muscle. Its head sits low between the shoulders and is split by a huge wide mouth lined with pale fangs. Small yellow eyes sit above the mouth, with a small gold mark on the brow, and a pair of dark curved horns rises from the top of the head. Two large ridged gray horns sweep out from the sides of the head and curve forward. Broad leathery bat wings with dark ribs rise from its shoulders. Thick arms and legs end in long dark curved claws, and a pale tan band crosses the belly.
