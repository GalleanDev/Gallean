Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge squat demon with a bulky purple body of knotted muscle. The broad back is ridged with thick folds, and the head sits low between the shoulders with two large ridged gray horns showing above them. Broad leathery bat wings with dark ribs are raised behind the shoulders. Heavy arms hang at its sides and end in long dark curved claws, and the squat legs end in clawed feet. A long tail with a pale tan tip hangs between the legs and curves along the ground.
