Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a giant bird with its wings spread. A crest of white and gray spiky feathers rises from the back of its head, and long pale streaked feathers run down its nape. Mottled brown feathers cover its back and body. The wings carry brown coverts over long flight feathers barred in white and dark brown. Its legs are covered in brown scales and end in broad yellow-tan feet with dark curved talons.
