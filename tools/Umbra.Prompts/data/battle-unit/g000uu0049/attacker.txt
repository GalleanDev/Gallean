Three-quarter front view of a giant bird standing upright with its wings spread. A crest of white and gray spiky feathers rises from its head above a large hooked yellow beak and a dark eye. Pale gray-tan speckled feathers cover its throat and breast, and dark brown feathers cover its body. The wings carry brown coverts over long flight feathers barred in white and dark brown. Its legs are covered in brown scales and end in broad yellow-tan feet with dark curved talons.
