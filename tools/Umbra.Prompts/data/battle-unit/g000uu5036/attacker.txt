Three-quarter front view of a large spider with a rounded hairy abdomen of light brown streaked with dark brown. Its smooth brown cephalothorax sits low in front of the abdomen, fringed with dark bristles above a pair of curved dark fangs and a row of small black eyes. Eight long legs arch out from the body, their brown segments banded with pale bony joints, and each leg ends in a dark bristly tuft. Fine hairs cover the body, and the coat lightens along the top of the abdomen.
