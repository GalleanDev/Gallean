Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a griffin with a heavy shaggy brown ruff around its head. Pale tan tufts rise from the crown above the ruff. Broad wings of dark gray-brown feathers, banded with pale tan along the leading edge, spread from its shoulders and hang open to either side. Brown fur covers the body over paler tan stripes at the flank and haunches, the hind legs are furred and end in dark talons, and a long thin tail with a small tuft at the tip curves out behind.
