Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a young man in a pale gray padded tunic. Thick black hair covers the back of his head in a heavy mass down to the nape. The tunic is ribbed in close vertical folds across his back, reaching to mid-thigh over a plain black belt, and its long sleeves end at bare pale hands. Dark brown boots rise to his knees. He holds a long slender dark blade with a gold hilt, angled down and away from his body.
