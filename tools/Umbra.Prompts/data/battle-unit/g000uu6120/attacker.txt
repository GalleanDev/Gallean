Three-quarter front view of a young man in a pale gray padded tunic. Thick black hair falls in a heavy fringe over his brow, leaving only his pale nose and chin in view. The tunic is ribbed in close vertical folds, reaching to mid-thigh over a plain black belt, and its long sleeves end at bare pale hands. Dark brown boots rise to his knees. He holds a long slender dark blade with a gold hilt, angled down and away from his body.
