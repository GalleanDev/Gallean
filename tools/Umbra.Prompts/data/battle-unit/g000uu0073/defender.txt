Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged woman in dark red armor. Pale cream branching horns fork out from the top of her head, and a dark navy panel hangs down the back of it between the shoulders. A round red shoulder plate caps each shoulder. A ribbed red strip runs down the middle of her back to the ground. Large wings spread wide, their deep red membranes stretched over dark spiked leading edges. Below the wings a tan robe with dark vertical stripes falls in long folds to the ground, and a long dark red pole crosses behind her from one shoulder to the ground.
