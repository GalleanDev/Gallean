Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a gaunt hunched creature with dark violet skin, crouched low on long spindly limbs. The back of its small head is sunk between raised shoulders. A matted mane of brown and tan fur covers its shoulders, and a long shroud of ragged black cloth hangs down its back to the knees.
