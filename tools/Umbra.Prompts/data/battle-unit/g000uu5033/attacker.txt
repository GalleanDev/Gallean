Three-quarter front view of a gaunt hunched creature with dark violet skin, crouched low on long spindly limbs. Its small head is sunk between raised shoulders, with two bright yellow eyes in a shrunken face. A matted mane of brown and tan fur covers its shoulders and upper chest. Dull red streaks run along its thin arms and legs, and a long shroud of ragged black cloth hangs behind its arms to the knees. Gray wrappings bind its wrists and ankles. Its long fingers end in curved claws, and its broad splayed feet spread flat on the ground.
