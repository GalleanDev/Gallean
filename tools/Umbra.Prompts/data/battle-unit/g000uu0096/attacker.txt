Three-quarter front view of an armored rider on a horse. The rider wears black plate armor of overlapping ridged scales with huge rounded black pauldrons, and a black helm crowned with a ring of pale bone skulls. He holds a long dark blade level across the horse. The horse is armored to match, with a black and pale bone chamfron over its head, two huge pale bone ribbed horns curling forward from it, and a ridged black plate with gold edging across its chest. A deep purple pleated caparison with gold trim hangs from the saddle to the ground, and black armored legs end in black hooves. A pale white cloth hangs at the rider's side.
