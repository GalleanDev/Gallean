Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an armored rider on a horse. A deep purple pleated caparison with gold trim covers the horse from the saddle to the ground and fills most of the view. Above it the rider wears black plate armor of overlapping ridged scales with huge rounded black pauldrons, and the back of a black helm crowned with a ring of pale bone skulls sits over a green and gold ornament at the collar. Two huge pale bone ribbed horns curl out past the shoulder. A long dark blade points away to the side, a pale white cloth hangs at the rider's side, and black hooves show below the hem.
