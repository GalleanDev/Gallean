Three-quarter front view of a lean man bent far forward over his knees. A thick curtain of reddish-brown dreadlocked hair falls forward over his bowed head and hides it. He wears a black sleeveless tunic with olive-gold trim, and a shoulder piece of dark plates banded with olive-gold sits on one shoulder. His arms and lower legs are bare pale skin. A heavy chain of pale links hangs from his hand at his hip. Dark wraps cover his thighs, a gray metal cuff closes above one bare foot, and a dark boot covers the other. He holds a slim straight blade with a wrapped grip.
