Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall shrouded figure. A deep red hood covers the back of its head, and a ribbed dark brown collar stands behind the neck between dark olive-black armor mottled with green on the shoulders. Large curved bone horns of pale tan rise from the shoulders on both sides. A deep red cloak falls from the shoulders in heavy vertical folds all the way to the ground, and the dark olive-black robe shows at the hem below it.
