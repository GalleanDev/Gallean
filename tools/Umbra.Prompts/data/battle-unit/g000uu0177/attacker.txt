Three-quarter front view of a tall shrouded figure with a bone skull for a head. A deep red hood covers the crown, and green eyes sit deep in its sockets above pale white strands that hang from the skull. Below the eyes the face falls away into the shadow inside the hood: no mouth, jaw line or teeth are visible there, only darkness, and the head stays perfectly still. Large curved bone horns of pale tan rise from the shoulders and arc across the chest. Dark olive-black armor mottled with green covers the chest and arms, and a deep red cloak hangs in long ragged strips at one side. Below the waist a dark olive-black robe falls to the ground in heavy folds, covering the legs and feet.
