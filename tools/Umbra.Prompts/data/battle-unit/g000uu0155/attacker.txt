Three-quarter front view of an armored rider on a horse. The rider wears a dark green hooded mantle, and long white hair falls around his tanned face. The horse carries green and gold head armor with round eye cups and a tapering nose plate over a plated neck. A dark green caparison with cream and gold embroidered bands covers the horse from the shoulders to the ground, parted at the front and edged with a broad cream border. He holds a long sword with a brown grip and a gray blade across his body, and dark hooves show below the hem.
