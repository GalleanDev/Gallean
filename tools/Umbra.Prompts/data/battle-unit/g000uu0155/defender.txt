Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an armored rider on a horse. He wears a dark green hooded mantle, and long white hair covers the back of his head. The horse's green and gold head armor rises beyond him, two pointed ears above a banded crest and a plated neck. A pale gray tail hangs between the panels, and dark hooves show below the hem. A long sword with a brown grip and a gray blade reaches past his shoulder.
