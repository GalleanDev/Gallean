Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge hunched beast with glossy deep red hide, ridged and heavily muscled. Its horned head turns to one side at the top of a thick neck, so the long jaw line breaks the outline of the shoulders. A crest of long flat tan horns sweeps back from the skull and runs the whole length of the spine in overlapping plates. The broad back and haunches are banded with the same red hide, a thick ribbed tail as broad as the body curls away along the ground, and long black claws spread from the feet.
