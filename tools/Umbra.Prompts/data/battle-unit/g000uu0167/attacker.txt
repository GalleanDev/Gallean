Three-quarter front view of a hulking winged gargoyle of black stone. Its body is craggy and plated in dark blue-black rock, with jagged spurs along its shoulders and back. Broad bat wings with ribbed struts and a scalloped upper edge spread behind it. A low head sits between its shoulders, wrapped in thick curling black horns, and one small yellow eye shows in the dark stone of its face. Thick arms end in heavy clawed hands, and it stands on short bent legs with wide clawed feet.
