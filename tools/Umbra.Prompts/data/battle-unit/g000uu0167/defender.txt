Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking winged gargoyle of black stone. Its craggy plated back is dark blue-black rock, ridged with jagged spurs along its shoulders and set with a lighter gray plate of rounded hollows between the wing joints. Broad bat wings with ribbed struts and a scalloped upper edge spread wide behind it. A thick tail hangs down and curls up at its blunt tip. It stands on short bent legs with wide clawed feet.
