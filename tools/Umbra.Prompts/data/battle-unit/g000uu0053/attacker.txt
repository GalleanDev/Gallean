Three-quarter front view of a burly man hunched forward. Shaggy black hair falls over a heavy dark beard, and a broad nose and bared teeth show above it. A ragged pale gray fur collar sits at his neck, and a large brown leather pauldron covers one shoulder above his bare tanned arms. Cream cloth shows at his chest above a dark belt. Brown leather skirt panels hang over red cloth breeches. A steel knee cop covers one knee and banded boots cover his feet. He holds two long axes with dark hafts and pale steel heads.
