Three-quarter front view of a heavily muscled humanoid figure with smooth olive-gold skin, its body ending at the waist in a rounded stump with no legs. The broad chest and ridged stomach are deeply cut, and dark seams run between the muscles. Its head is a smooth, featureless sculpted dome with a narrow ridge running down its front; it has no face, no eyes, no mouth and no beak. Long thick arms reach out, tapering to narrow wrists and long bony fingers with dark claws.
