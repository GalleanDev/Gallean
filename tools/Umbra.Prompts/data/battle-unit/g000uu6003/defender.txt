Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a heavily muscled humanoid figure with smooth olive-gold skin, its body ending at the waist in a rounded stump with no legs. The broad back is deeply cut, and dark seams run between the muscles down a thick spine. Its head is a smooth sculpted dome with a flat crown above a heavy neck, and the blunt beak juts out past the side of it. Long thick arms reach out, tapering to narrow wrists and long bony fingers with dark claws.
