Three-quarter front view of a heavy-set bald man with pale gray skin, bare to the waist. Black war paint rings his deep-set eyes. A large quilted dark brown pauldron studded with pale rivets covers one shoulder, and a dark leather strap crosses his chest with a round metal boss and small iron fittings hanging from it. A wide olive-brown belt with a metal buckle holds a dark red loincloth that hangs between bare pale thighs. Brown leather wrappings bind his forearm, and tall brown wrapped boots leave his pale toes bare. A banded steel guard covers one knee. He holds a broad pale steel axe on a long dark haft.
