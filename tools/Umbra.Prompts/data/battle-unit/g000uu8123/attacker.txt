Three-quarter front view of an elf man in brass-gold banded armor, with a long narrow face and long pointed ears. A very large mane of orange and pale gold hair rises from his head and sweeps back in ragged points, filling the space above him and to the left of the frame in long separate strands. Only the lower front of his face shows clear of the mane, pale against it, set low and turned toward the right of the frame; the rest of his head is covered by hair, and one pointed ear runs back into it. His mouth is closed and his head is still. Rounded gold shoulder caps sit above arms plated in the same metal, ending in bare pale hands. Gold plates cover his thighs and shins above gold sandals. A red panel hangs from a gold belt down the front of his legs, and a large red cloak with a broad gold border falls from his shoulders past his side. He holds a dark bow with a broad gray blade at its upper limb across his body.
