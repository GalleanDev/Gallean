Three-quarter front view of a lizard man standing with his legs apart. His hide is mottled dark green and yellow-green and scaled all over. A long reptilian snout carries a row of white teeth, and a pale yellow eye sits above it in a low ridged skull. Brown leather straps cross his bare chest and meet at a pale bone-white disc above a brown belt. One thigh and shin are cased in dark brown segmented plates, and a brown banded bracer wraps a forearm. He stands on broad clawed feet with pale claws and holds two long slender curved swords with small hilts.
