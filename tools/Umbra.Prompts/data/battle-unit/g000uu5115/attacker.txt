Three-quarter front view of a huge hunched ogre with gray skin. A smooth pale gray dome covers the top of his head, with dark hair tufts and large ears beside small dark eyes under a heavy brow, a gold ring through his nose and a wide row of teeth. Huge ribbed black shoulder guards fan over his shoulders above a dark brown riveted breastplate. A wide brown belt with a square brass buckle holds a studded brown leather kilt over his thighs, and brown wrappings sit above bare gray feet with dark nails. A long curved pale gray shape hangs from one shoulder. He grips a huge pale bone club and a thin dark shaft with a small spiked head.
