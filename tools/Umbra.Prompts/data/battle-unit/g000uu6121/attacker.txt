Three-quarter front view of a woman in dark gray armor. A dark gray helm with red curling ridges across its front encloses her head. A dark gray molded breastplate with red scrollwork covers her chest, and banded white and dark gray sleeves run down her arms. A red pauldron with a long red drape sits on one shoulder. A white cloth is wrapped at the waist above a wide red belt with black scrollwork and a white pleated skirt panel. Dark gray greaves and red knee guards sit above dark gray boots with red trim. She holds a long dark polearm with a broad red blade at each end.
