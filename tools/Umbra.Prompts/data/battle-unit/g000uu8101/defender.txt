Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a gaunt horned demon with dark bronze hide. Long dark horns curve back from the head beside a tall straight horn on the crown and thin whip-like feelers. Curved bone spikes rise over the shoulders above a ridged back set with pale bone plates down the spine, and long sinewy arms end in clawed hands. A pale tan cloth wraps the hips. A long ringed tail sweeps to the ground behind jagged plates at the hips and knees, long jointed legs and broad three-toed clawed feet. He holds a great axe with a broad crescent blade and a brass-ringed haft.
