Three-quarter front view of a gaunt horned demon with dark bronze hide. Blank white eyes and a wide fanged mouth sit in a broad head, and long dark horns curve back from it beside a tall straight horn on the crown and thin whip-like feelers. A bare muscular chest with ridged ribs narrows to a lean waist. Curved bone spikes sweep down from the shoulders across the chest, and long sinewy arms end in clawed hands. Jagged plates cover the hips and knees of long jointed legs standing on broad three-toed clawed feet. He holds a great axe with a broad crescent blade and a brass-ringed haft.
