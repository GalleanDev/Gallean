Three-quarter front view of a large aquatic beast with a rounded plated body and a long thick neck. Its hide is dark blue-green and scaled, marbled with paler blue and olive streaks. A crest of stiff olive-yellow spines and a few straight dark quills stands along the back of its ridged head. Its bony head narrows to a blunt snout above small sunken eyes. Ribbed pale yellow plates run down the throat and belly. Two broad ray-finned flippers of olive and dark gray spread from its sides, and a webbed olive fin hangs below. A long tapering tail curves up behind the body, tipped with a small pale fin.
