Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large aquatic beast with a rounded plated body and a long thick neck. Its hide is dark blue-green and scaled, marbled with paler blue and olive streaks. A crest of stiff olive-yellow spines and a few straight dark quills stands along the back of its ridged head and down the nape. Broad overlapping scale plates cover the humped back, veined with pale lines. Two broad ray-finned flippers of olive and dark gray spread from its sides, and a webbed olive fin hangs below. A long tapering tail curves around behind the body.
