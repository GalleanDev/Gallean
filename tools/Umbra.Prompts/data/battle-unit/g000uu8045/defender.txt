Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad-shouldered man in shaggy brown hide and fur. A brown fur cap covers the back of his head above a fall of dark red-brown hair. Ragged copper-brown fur tufts edge the shoulders, hips, knees and boots. A wide tan band wraps one upper arm above a black leather sleeve, and black leather straps cross his back. A rounded gray plate sits at the small of his back above a dark brown hide kilt, brown leggings and gray knee caps. He holds a long harpoon with a gray shaft and a cluster of barbed gray prongs.
