Three-quarter front view of a robed man. His head is wrapped in dark green cloth under a tall pointed dark green hat with a wide brim, and a small gold boss sits at its side. The brim is pulled so low that no face shows beneath it, only deep shadow between the brim and the fur mantle. He holds a gnarled brown branching staff upright. A brown fur mantle with gold scrollwork covers his shoulders over a dark green sleeve with gold stripes, a wide dark red cuff and a white gathered cuff. His white robe is laced with dark red cords across the chest and falls to a gold-patterned hem band above red-brown shoes. A dark green cloak with a wide gold embroidered border hangs behind him to the ground.
