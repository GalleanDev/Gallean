Three-quarter front view of a hulking horned beast hunched forward on heavy muscular arms. Its coarse dark gray hide is thick and plated over the chest and limbs. A pair of long ridged copper-brown horns sweep up and back from the head, which hangs low between the shoulders with a long dark snout and one small red eye. Shaggy black bristles ruff the neck and stand out at the shoulders and elbows. Curved pale gray spikes jut from the shoulders. A red curling mark is drawn across the chest, and the legs end in heavy clawed feet.
