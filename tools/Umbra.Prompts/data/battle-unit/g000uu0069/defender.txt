Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking horned beast hunched forward on heavy muscular arms. Its coarse dark gray hide is thick and plated over the broad ridged back, which rises in a hard shell between the shoulders. A pair of long ridged copper-brown horns sweep up and over that shell from the head below it. Shaggy black bristles cover the shell and stand out at the shoulders and elbows, and curved pale gray spikes jut from the shoulders. A long segmented tail hangs down behind, and the legs end in heavy clawed feet.
