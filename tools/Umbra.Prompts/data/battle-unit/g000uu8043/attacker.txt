Three-quarter front view of a slender witch in a long black gown, her head turned toward her fan. Her face is mostly hidden: blue-black hair falls over her cheek and the shadow of her curved black and tan horns lies across it, so only a sliver of warm ivory skin shows, the same tone as her bare throat and chest; no bright white skin shows anywhere on her head. A large white openwork fan rises beside her shoulder. A dark red sash crosses a black bodice, and the gown flares to the floor over a deep red underskirt with silver-white rosettes down its sides.
