Three-quarter front view of a large black winged beast with a ridged stone carapace. Curved horns sweep back from its head above a blunt snout, and a crest of upright spikes stands along its skull. Its yellow eyes sit deep beneath a heavy ridge. Overlapping plates armor its shoulders, chest and back, and short spikes stand along them. Two large membranous wings open from its shoulders, one raised high and one spread low. It crouches on thick ridged limbs that end in broad clawed feet, and heavy claws tip its hands.
