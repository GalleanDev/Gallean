Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large black winged beast with a ridged stone carapace. Two large membranous wings open wide from its shoulders, their outer edges falling in deep scallops. Overlapping plates armor its back and short spikes stand along them. The back of its horned head rises between the wing shoulders, topped by a crest of upright spikes. A long thick tail curves down from its hips and tapers to a flat point, and its thick ridged limbs end in broad clawed feet.
