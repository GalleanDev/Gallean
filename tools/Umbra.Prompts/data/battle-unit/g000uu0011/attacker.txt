Three-quarter front view of a hooded man in a long blue robe. A deep blue hood covers his head, with a gold band at its edge, and thick auburn hair spills from under it across his face, hiding one eye and his cheek; he is young and beardless. A wide blue shoulder cape with a white and gold border spreads over his shoulders, and a round gold clasp sits at the throat with a pale strip hanging below it. The bright blue robe falls in deep vertical pleats below a brown leather belt, and gold bands run above the pale hem. His hands are in brown leather gloves and rest together at his waist, and dark shoes show beneath the hem.
