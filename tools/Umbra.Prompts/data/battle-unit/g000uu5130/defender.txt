Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall figure wrapped in a heavy brown cloak. A pointed brown hood is drawn up, its crown worked with faded ocher patterning and its rim edged in pale gray. His head is high inside the top of the hood, turned aside; just under the hood's rim show a sharp nose, narrow eyes and a hard, clean-shaven jaw, above the shoulder cape. A short shoulder cape is bordered with the same pale gray and clasped at the throat by a small red bead. A white ruffled collar sits above dark layered leather armor and a dark red under-robe. The long cloak falls in deep vertical folds to the ground, its hem banded with worn gold embroidery. A slim steel blade juts out from under the cloak above brown boots.
