Three-quarter front view of a tall figure wrapped head to foot in a heavy brown cloak. A pointed brown hood is drawn up over the head, its crown worked with faded ocher patterning and its rim edged in pale gray. A short shoulder cape falls over the shoulders, bordered with the same pale gray and clasped at the throat by a small red bead. From the border hangs a tapering brown tongue of cloth tipped with small pale beads. The long cloak falls in deep vertical folds to the ground, its hem banded with worn gold embroidery. A slim steel blade juts out from under the cloak.
