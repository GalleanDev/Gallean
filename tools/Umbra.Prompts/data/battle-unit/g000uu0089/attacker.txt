Three-quarter front view of a skeleton in bone armor and a deep red cloak. A red cloth cap banded with bone sits on its bare skull, and two long curved bone horns sweep up from the shoulders. Curved rib bones are lashed together into plates over the shoulders and chest. The deep red cloak hangs in tattered strips over a black robe, and pale arm bones show at the sides. Black boots with gold trim stand below, and it holds a long curved silver blade.
