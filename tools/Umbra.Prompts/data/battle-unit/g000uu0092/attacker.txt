Three-quarter front view of a hulking hunched wolf-beast standing on two legs. Its hide is dark brown and mottled with gray, and thick matted gray-brown fur covers its head and shoulders and hangs down its back in long strands. A broad muzzle juts from the fur below a single yellow eye. Heavy muscled arms end in huge hands with long blue-gray claws, and its broad feet carry the same claws. Dirty white cloth wrappings are wound around one forearm and the other wrist.
