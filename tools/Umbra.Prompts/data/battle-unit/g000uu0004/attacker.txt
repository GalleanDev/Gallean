Three-quarter front view of a man in a long dark brown cloak with a red lining. A tall dark brown hat with a wide floppy brim sits low over his head, a pale gray feather stuck through its band. A thick red scarf is wound high around his neck and up to the brim, and between the scarf and the brim there is only solid black shadow, with nothing visible beneath the hat. A dark blue pauldron with gold scrollwork sits on one shoulder, and a ragged tuft of pale bone-colored quills hangs beside it. The cloak opens over a dark red robe of narrow vertical folds that falls to the ground, with red trim along its edges. Gray round-toed boots show at the hem. He holds a long straight steel sword angled down.
