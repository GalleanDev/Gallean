Three-quarter front view of a young woman with a lithe, athletic build in gray hide and fur. A white animal skull with a shaggy gray and white pelt is worn over her head like a hood, its jaws above her brow, and the pelt falls around her face and past her shoulders. Between it her skin is tanned, with a broad band of red paint across her eyes, and her mouth is closed and still. Two branching dark twigs strung with bright red feathers rise behind her shoulders. A pale gray hide mantle and a short sleeveless hide vest cover her chest, leaving her toned midriff and waist bare above a wide dark band low on her hips, and her long bare legs show below it. A red stone hangs at her chest. Coiled gray metal rings wrap her shins above shaggy pale fur boots. One hand holds a pale bone rod with a red tip.
