Three-quarter front view of a stocky man in a red banded coat. A rounded brown helmet with a wide neck flap covers his head above a brown beard. A pale gray scarf hangs at his neck. The coat is striped with vertical brown-gold bands above a skirt of brown leather lames. A rounded pauldron of banded steel and brown leather sits on one shoulder, a banded steel sleeve covers one arm and a brown leather sleeve the other, and brown gauntlets cover the hands. Brown boots with pale steel caps cover his feet. He holds a long-hafted axe with a broad pale steel head.
