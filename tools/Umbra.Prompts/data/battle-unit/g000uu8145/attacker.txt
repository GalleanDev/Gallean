Three-quarter front view of a broad-shouldered man in shaggy brown hide and fur. A brown fur cap is pulled low over his brow, and a stiff leather half-mask covers his nose, mouth and jaw, so only his eyes and the strip of weathered skin beneath the cap show above it. A long braided brown beard falls from under the mask over his chest. Ragged copper-brown fur tufts edge the shoulders, hips, knees and boots, and two white curved spikes sit in the brown hide of one shoulder. A ribbed gray plate covers the belly under black leather straps, and a wide tan band wraps one upper arm above a black leather sleeve. A brown leather belt with a tan buckle holds a dark brown hide kilt over brown leggings and gray knee caps. He holds a long harpoon with a gray shaft and a cluster of barbed gray prongs.
