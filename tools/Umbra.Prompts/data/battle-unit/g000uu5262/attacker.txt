Three-quarter front view of a tall man in long robes. The bleached skull of a ram is worn over his head like a helm, its long narrow snout pointing down over his face and its thick curled horns sweeping back and around, and his own face is hidden inside the skull. A twist of dark red cloth sits at his throat. A black mantle with olive-gold trim hangs over his shoulders, open over a banded olive and dark panel at the chest. Beneath it a loose off-white robe falls to the ground in heavy folds, its hem banded in olive-brown and dark stripes. Wide sleeves end at bare pale hands with long fingers.
