Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall man in long robes. Two pale horns curve up and back above the back of his head, over long black hair, and a twist of dark red cloth shows at his neck. A black mantle with olive-gold trim covers his shoulders and upper back, its trimmed edge falling in points. Beneath it a loose off-white robe falls to the ground in heavy folds, its hem banded in olive-brown and dark stripes. A wide sleeve hangs at his side.
