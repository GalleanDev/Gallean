Three-quarter front view of a heavy-set giant in a brown leather jerkin. Dark shaggy hair and a thick dark beard frame a ruddy face with a broad hooked nose. A collar of pale gray fur lies across his shoulders above the jerkin, banded with pale gray. A white shirt with wide sleeves shows beneath it, and a green cloth panel runs down to a wide brown belt with a gold buckle. Bright green breeches cover his thighs above pale cloth wrappings at the knees. Bare muscular forearms end in broad hands, and he holds a thick rough log of brown wood. Dark brown boots with studded straps cover his feet.
