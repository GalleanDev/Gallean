Three-quarter front view of a huge broad-shouldered man with pale gray-white hide. A shaggy wolf-like head sits low between his shoulders, with a heavy brow, deep-set pale eyes, a broad flat nose and a thick gray beard that spreads over his chest. Pointed ears stand up through the fur. Smooth rounded caps of the same pale gray cover his shoulders above a knotted gray pelt across the chest. A gold strap crosses the body to a wide gold belt and skirt with a round gold boss. Shaggy gray fringes hang at his forearms and above broad bare feet with dark claws.
