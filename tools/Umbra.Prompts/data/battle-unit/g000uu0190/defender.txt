Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge broad-shouldered man with pale gray-white hide. A shaggy gray mane covers the back of his head, low between smooth rounded caps of the same pale gray that cover his shoulders. His broad back is knotted with heavy muscle, and a gold strap runs across it from one shoulder down to a wide gold belt and skirt with a round gold boss at the back. Shaggy gray fringes hang at his forearms and above broad bare feet.
