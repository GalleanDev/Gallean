Three-quarter front view of a hulking orc with olive-green skin in dark iron plate. Pale curved horns rise from his dark helm and from his spiked pauldrons, and a black spiky crest stands above a dark maroon shaggy mane that covers his shoulders. His olive face has a heavy tusked jaw and a row of pale teeth. Segmented dark iron plates cover his chest, hips and shins, with thick brown leather wraps on his forearms and thighs. Cream wraps bind his olive-green feet. A dark gauntlet with long blade-like claws covers one hand, and a steel chain hangs from it.
