Three-quarter front view of a tall red demon wrapped in long ribbed folds of deep red. Two long pale tan horns, ridged along their length, curve up and back from its dark head, and a small yellow eye and short pale tusks show below them. Heavy folds hang from its shoulders and fall in ribbed columns to the ground. Bat wings of dark red membrane spread behind it on red ribs, one wide and one raised at the shoulder.
