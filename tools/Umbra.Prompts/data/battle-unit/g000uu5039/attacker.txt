Three-quarter front view of a wolf standing low on all fours, its head dropped between the shoulders. Its coat is dark gray and black, thick and shaggy, with a shaggy ruff around the neck and a jagged crest of raised black hackles along the neck and shoulders. Tall pointed ears with pale tips stand on the dark head, and yellow eyes sit above a long gray snout with a pale silver underside and a black nose. The lean body narrows to pale gray legs with white paws, banded with darker gray.
