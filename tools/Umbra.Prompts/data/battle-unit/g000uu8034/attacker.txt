Three-quarter front view of a slender woman in a long black gown covered in swirling gold filigree. A wild mass of spiky pale straw-blond hair stands out around her head above a black band across her brow, and below it her small face is lightly tanned, with a closed mouth. Her pointed ears show at the edge of the hair. She has bare shoulders and arms, with black gloves on her hands and a ribbed bronze cuff at one wrist. The gown falls to the floor with a high slit that bares one leg, and black boots show beneath.
