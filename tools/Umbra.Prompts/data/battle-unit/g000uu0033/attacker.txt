Three-quarter front view of a lean, athletic young huntress in fur-trimmed leather armor, her face angular like a wild cat's: hollow cheeks under high cheekbones, a narrow tapering jaw, a pointed chin and amber eyes, her skin lightly tanned, her mouth closed. Long wavy auburn hair, golden copper at the ends, falls from a studded brown headband down both sides of her face and past her shoulders, and small pointed ears show at its edges. A cream fur mantle covers a dark brown jerkin and gray steel chest plate, above red puffed breeches and fur-cuffed brown boots.
