Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large bear on all fours, low and heavy-set. Its coat is dark chocolate brown, thick and coarse, warming to a reddish tan on the lower legs. The broad rump fills the view, with a short dark tail set high on it, and loose folds of skin ripple over the shoulders and flanks. A high shoulder hump rises ahead of the rump, and the lowered head shows only the back of the skull and one small rounded ear past it. The heavy legs end in wide paws with short dark claws.
