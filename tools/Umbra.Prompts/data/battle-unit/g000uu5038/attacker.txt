Three-quarter front view of a large bear on all fours, low and heavy-set. Its coat is dark chocolate brown, thick and coarse, warming to a reddish tan on the lower legs. Loose folds of skin ripple over the shoulders and flanks. A high shoulder hump rises over a broad lowered head with small rounded ears set well back, a tapering brown muzzle, a black nose and small dark eyes. A few small yellowed teeth show at the jaw. The heavy legs end in wide paws with short dark claws.
