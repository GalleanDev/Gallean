Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a woman in armor riding a gray horse. A dark rounded steel helm with an engraved band shows at the back of her head, with pale feathered wings rising from each side of the helm. A shaggy mass of tangled dark brown strands covers her shoulders and the whole body of the mount. A large pale feathered wing with a smooth gray scalloped upper edge rises behind them, and two long slender straight blades with green gems cross in front of it. A pale cloth skirt with a gold band along its hem hangs beneath the mass. The horse turns its long gray muzzle to the side, and its legs end in dark hooves.
