Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a crouching purple demon with ribbed hide streaked in pale violet. The back of its crested head sits between heavy shoulders, and ridged plates run down its spine. Broad wings spread from its back, their purple ribbed membranes fanned on struts tipped with black hooked spurs. Two long ribbed tails taper to the ground behind it. A long dark shaft juts out at its side, and it stands on broad clawed feet.
