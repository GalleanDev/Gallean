Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking figure in dark green armor with a knotted, corroded surface. Long pale gray hair falls over the back of its head and down between rounded dark green pauldrons that cover the huge shoulders. Below them a red and orange banded sash crosses the upper back, and ragged pale tan cloth hangs in long torn strips from the shoulders to the ground, covering the back and legs. Dark green shins and clawed feet show between the strips. A dark mace with a rounded head on a long shaft rises at one side.
