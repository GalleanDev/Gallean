Three-quarter front view of a hulking figure in dark green armor with a knotted, corroded surface. Its head is a small skull of bare bone, bowed forward and hidden almost completely behind a curtain of long straight silver-gray hair that falls from the crown past the cheekbones; only the jaw and the upper teeth show below the hair, and a dim point of green light sits deep in each eye socket behind it. The jaw is closed and the head is still. Rounded dark green pauldrons cover the huge shoulders above a ribbed chest and belly of the same green. Ragged pale tan cloth hangs in long torn strips from the shoulders, the arms and the waist, where a dark belt with a heavy buckle crosses the body. One hand holds a dark mace with a rounded head on a long shaft. Green clawed feet show below the strips of cloth.
