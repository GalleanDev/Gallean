Three-quarter front view of a heavy man in deep red segmented plate armor. Long pale gray hair falls around his gaunt gray-white face, and his sunken red eyes sit deep in dark hollows. A high collar of pale bone segments covers his chest above ribbed red bands of belly and leg armor. Red pauldrons with dark studs cap his shoulders, and pale bone-colored gauntlets carry long curved steel claws on both hands. A deep red cloak with a high ribbed collar and a black lining rises behind him and falls to the ground. Gray clawed feet show below the armor.
