Three-quarter front view of a broad-shouldered man leaning forward in dark leather armor. Short black hair sits above a pale gray band across his eyes, with a thin strap that reaches back around his head. His face is narrow and gaunt, sharp cheekbones over sunken cheeks. A deep purple scarf is wound over his neck and chin and hangs across one shoulder. His cuirass is banded dark brown leather with straps and studs over a wide plated belt, and heavy brown leather gauntlets cover his forearms. Baggy dark brown trousers are tucked into tall dark boots with wide cuffs. He holds a long curved saber with a broad pale steel blade.
