Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad-shouldered man leaning forward in dark leather armor. Short black hair covers the back of his head above a pale gray band and a thin strap. A deep purple scarf is wound around his neck and hangs across one shoulder and down his back. His cuirass is banded dark brown leather with straps and studs over a wide plated belt, and heavy brown leather gauntlets cover his forearms. Baggy dark brown trousers are tucked into tall dark boots with wide cuffs. He holds a long curved saber with a broad pale steel blade.
