Three-quarter front view of a hunched undead brute with dark red-brown skin and wild white hair. His face is a dried skull covered in leathery skin, shadowed under a heavy brow: two faint red pinpricks glow deep in the dark sockets, and a noseless flat ridge runs down to a long gaping mouth, frozen open, head still. There are no bright eyes and no eye whites; everything between the hair and the mouth is dark red-brown and shadow. Pale beads ring his neck. Pale tan bandages bound by gray straps wrap his forearms and shins, and black rags hang from one shoulder.
