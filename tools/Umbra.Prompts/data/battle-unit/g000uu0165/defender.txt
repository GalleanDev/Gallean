Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a very short broad dwarf whose head is covered by a smooth golden domed helm. A white pleated cloak falls over his back and shoulders, banded with gold. A brown ribbed barrel is strapped at one shoulder, and a spotted cream hide hangs at one side. A gnarled dark brown branch carved with runes crosses above him, and a dark curved axe blade on a long brown haft rests at his side.
