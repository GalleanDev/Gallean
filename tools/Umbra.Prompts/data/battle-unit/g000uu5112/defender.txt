Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking orc with olive-green skin in dark iron plate. Pale curved horns rise from his dark helm and from his spiked pauldrons, and a black spiky crest stands above a dark maroon shaggy mane that covers his shoulders and upper back. Segmented dark iron plates cover his back, hips and shins, with thick brown leather wraps on his forearms and thighs, and a pale bone-white plate sits at the small of his back. Cream wraps bind his olive-green feet. A dark gauntlet with long blade-like claws covers one hand.
