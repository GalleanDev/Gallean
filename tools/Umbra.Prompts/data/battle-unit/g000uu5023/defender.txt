Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged dragon with a deep green scaled hide. Its horned head turns in profile at the top of a ridged neck, showing a crest of golden-tan spikes and a long green snout. A mane of golden-tan fronds runs down the neck and along the spine. Large leathery wings spread from its back in deep green, ribbed and mottled with darker green. It stands on four legs banded in darker green and tipped with golden-tan claws, and a long tapering tail trails behind it, tufted with more golden-tan fronds.
