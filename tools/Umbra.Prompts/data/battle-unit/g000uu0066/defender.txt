Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall winged demon in a long red and black robe. A smooth black dome covers the back of the head, and a pair of pale bone-colored horns curve up and over it. Huge red and black shoulder plates flare out to either side of it. Large bat wings spread behind, their dark red membranes streaked with black and ribbed with heavy struts. The robe falls straight to the ground in a broad train, its dark gray back marked all over with red curling scrollwork.
