Three-quarter front view of a tall winged demon in a long red and black robe. A smooth black dome covers the head, and a pair of pale bone-colored horns curve up and over it. Huge red and black shoulder plates flare out to either side, with pale ivory spikes jutting from beneath them. A pale bone-colored ribbed column runs down the center of the ribbed dark red chest. Large bat wings rise behind the shoulders, their brown and orange membranes ribbed with dark struts. A clawed red hand shows at one side, and the robe falls in stiff dark red pleats with black ribbing to the ground.
