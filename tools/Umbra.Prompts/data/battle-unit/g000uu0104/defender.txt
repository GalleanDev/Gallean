Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a towering many-armed figure whose body tapers into a long bell-shaped shell. The shell is dark bronze-brown and crowded with raised skulls and bones in bands, with a round medallion low on the back and a ragged fringe along the hem. A heavy cowl of dark bone plates covers the head between broad plated shoulders. Many long thin arms of segmented bone spread out from the shoulders and end in splayed skeletal hands with long fingers. A small dark weight hangs on a chain from one of them.
