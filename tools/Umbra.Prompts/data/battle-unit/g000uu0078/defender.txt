Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a woman in ragged pale gray wrappings. Long streaming pale gray hair covers the back of her head and falls in loose strands down her shoulders. Her arms are spread wide, and wide ragged sleeves of pale gray cloth hang from them with dark torn edges. Pale hands show at the ends of the sleeves. A long pale gray shroud hangs down her back in soft folds and frays into hanging strips at the bottom.
