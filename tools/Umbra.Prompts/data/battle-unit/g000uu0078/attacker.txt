Three-quarter front view of a woman in ragged pale gray wrappings. Long streaming pale gray hair sweeps back from her head. A pale cloth is wrapped over the lower face, and green eyes show in white skin above it. A necklace of dark round beads hangs across her chest. Her arms are spread wide, and wide ragged sleeves of pale gray cloth hang from them with dark torn edges. Pale hands show at the ends of the sleeves. A long pale gray shroud falls from her waist and frays into hanging strips.
