Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean older man in a green robe with wide copper-brown trim. Shaggy white hair with dark gray underneath spreads over the back of his head and down to his shoulders. The robe reaches his knees and is gathered by a broad copper-brown belt, and its hem carries the same copper-brown band. His arms are bare and tanned below wide copper-brown cuffs, and his legs are bare and tanned down to bare feet, with black cords wrapped below one knee.
