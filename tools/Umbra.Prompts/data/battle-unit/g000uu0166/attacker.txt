Three-quarter front view of a winged man in black segmented plate armor veined with red. Two long pale horns curve up from his gray head, with smaller horns beside them. Violet eyes sit under a heavy ridged brow. Bone-white plates cover his collar and belly, and more of them cap his knees and shins. Wide bat wings spread from his shoulders, their pale cream membranes ribbed with dark brown struts and clawed at the tips. His clawed feet end in pale talons. He holds a long dark blade level across his body.
