Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged man in black segmented plate armor veined with red. Pale horns curve up from his head, and long black hair falls between his shoulders. Wide bat wings spread from his shoulders, their gray-brown membranes ribbed with dark brown struts and clawed at the tips. A pale bone plate runs down his spine to a red mark at his waist, and bone-white plates cap the backs of his knees. His clawed feet end in pale talons, and a thin dark tail hangs behind them.
