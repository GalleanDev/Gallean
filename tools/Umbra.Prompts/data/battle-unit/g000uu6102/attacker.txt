Three-quarter front view of a hulking winged demon standing on two clawed legs. Its black plated hide has a blue sheen, with thin dark red seams between the plates. A mane of long backswept blade-like black horns fans out around a dark face with a pale fanged jaw. One broad bat wing spreads wide, its gray-brown membrane stretched between dark ribs, with hooked spines at the joints. Heavy shoulders and arms end in clawed hands with pale talons, the legs in heavy clawed feet with pale claws. A long tapering tail trails behind, and a furled pale gray feathered wing rises above the shoulder.
