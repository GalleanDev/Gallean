Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking winged demon standing on two clawed legs. Its black plated hide has a blue sheen, with thin dark red seams between the plates. A mane of long backswept blade-like black horns crowns the back of its head. One broad bat wing sweeps out behind, showing its dark blue-black outer membrane edged with dark red and hooked spines at the joints. The arms end in clawed hands with pale talons and the legs in heavy clawed feet with pale claws. A long tapering tail curves down behind, and a furled pale gray feathered wing lies along its back.
