Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a heavy centaur with dark brown skin and a dark brown horse body. A huge shaggy mane of straw-gold hair covers the back of his head and spills down his back. His arms are thick and bare, and his shoulders are broad and rounded. A long dark wooden club bound with cord rises behind his shoulder. A shaggy straw-gold tail hangs over the rump. Straw-gold tufts hang down one hind leg above heavy dark hooves.
