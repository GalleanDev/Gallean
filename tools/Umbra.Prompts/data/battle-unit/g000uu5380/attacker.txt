Three-quarter front view of a huge hunched beast with glossy deep red hide, ridged and heavily muscled. Its long low head juts forward on a thick neck, with a blunt red snout and two rows of long pale tan teeth along the jaws. A crest of long flat tan horns sweeps back from the skull and runs down the neck and back. Heavy forelimbs end in long black claws, and a thick ribbed tail as broad as the body curls around beside it.
