Three-quarter front view of a large bear on all fours, low and heavy-set. Its thick cream white fur is dense and slightly matted, with pale tan patches across the shoulders and haunches and faint rust-colored staining near the joints. A broad head with a heavy brow ridge and small rounded ears hangs low on a thick neck, tapering to a long muzzle with a black nose and small dark eyes. A heavy shoulder hump rises above the lowered head. The thick legs end in wide paws with short black claws.
