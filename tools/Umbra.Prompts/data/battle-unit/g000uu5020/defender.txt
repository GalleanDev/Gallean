Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged dragon with silver-white scales banded with gray. A swept-back crest of long slender blade-like horns rises from its head, turned aside to show a long narrow snout in profile. Large bat wings with silver-gray membranes spread wide from its shoulders, and a row of small pale spines runs along its back and down a long tapering banded tail. Four heavy legs end in tan claws.
