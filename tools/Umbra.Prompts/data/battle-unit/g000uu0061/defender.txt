Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge muscular winged demon. Its tan-brown hide is banded with dark stripes and patched with dark red scales at the shoulders, hips and thighs. Long ridged horns sweep back from the bony back of its head, and pale bone spikes stud its shoulders and run down its spine. Huge leathery wings of dark red membrane with pale ribs spread wide from its back, hooked with bone spurs along their upper edges. It holds a long-hafted axe with a broad curved steel blade, a thick striped tail hangs down behind its legs, and it stands on broad clawed feet.
