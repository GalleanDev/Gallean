Three-quarter front view of a huge muscular winged demon. Its tan-brown hide is banded with dark stripes and patched with dark red scales at the shoulders, hips and thighs. A bony head sits low between its shoulders, with long ridged horns sweeping back, bright green eyes and a wide mouth of long white fangs. Rows of pale bone spikes run down its chest and along its ribs. It holds a long-hafted axe with a broad curved steel blade. Huge leathery wings of dark red membrane with pale ribs open behind it, hooked with bone spurs, and a thick striped tail trails behind its clawed feet.
