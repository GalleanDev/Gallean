Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad heavy-shouldered humanoid figure covered from head to foot in matted shaggy dark green vegetation. The back of its head is a shaggy lump of the same growth, set low between the shoulders. Its shoulders and arms swell into great round balls of the same tangled growth that hang at its sides.
