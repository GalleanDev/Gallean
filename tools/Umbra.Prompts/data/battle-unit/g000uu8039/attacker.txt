Three-quarter front view of a broad heavy-shouldered humanoid figure covered from head to foot in matted shaggy dark green vegetation. A bone-white skull-like face sits in the mass at the top, with hollow dark sockets under a ridged brow and a wide dark gaping mouth. Its shoulders and arms swell into great round balls of the same tangled growth, one of them closed around a pale knot of bone-white wood pitted with dark holes. Thick shaggy legs carry it down to heavy rounded feet of pale mottled gray stone streaked with green.
