Three-quarter front view of a young man in worn work clothes. Brown hair falls in a straight fringe over his pale face. A white cloth collar sits at his neck above a dark maroon sleeveless tunic worn over a dark gray shirt. A pale gray segmented sleeve covers each arm and both hands are in dark brown gloves, with a brown leather pad at the shoulder. Dark charcoal trousers carry a pale patch at the thigh, and pale cloth wraps bound with brown straps cover his shins above dark brown shoes. He holds a long wooden pitchfork with three pale metal tines.
