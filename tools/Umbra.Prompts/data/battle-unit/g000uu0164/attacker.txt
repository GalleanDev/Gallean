Three-quarter front view of a hunched old dwarf, his head low between huge shoulders. Long shaggy white hair falls from his crown over his brow and past his cheeks into a thick white beard spread over his chest, so only a small patch of weathered tan face shows: a small short nose and two small eyes set close under white brows, one partly hidden behind falling white hair. A brown gray-mottled hide covers his back, shoulders and upper arms. His arms end in dark hands with long pale claws. A black kilt with yellow trim and a gold disc hangs at his waist above big bare tan clawed feet.
