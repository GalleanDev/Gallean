Three-quarter front view of a lean man in olive-green quilted armor ribbed with horizontal bands. A tall flat-topped olive-green hood sits on his head, with long pointed pale ears standing out to each side above a lean pale face, and a steel gorget rises around his neck. A steel breastplate covers his chest. A large rounded olive-green quilted pad sits on one shoulder, and a rounded steel pauldron caps the other above a steel-plated arm. Steel gauntlets end in pale fingers. Black straps cross his chest, and a narrow belt with a small gold buckle sits at his waist. Steel plates cover his thighs and shins, and he wears steel-capped boots. He holds a long dark steel bow with a broad leaf-shaped blade at each limb tip and a thin purple string.
