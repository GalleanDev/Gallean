Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad man in white plate armor. Dark hair covers the back of his head between huge rounded pauldrons of ribbed white steel. A long cloak hangs from his shoulders to the ground, its cream outer surface falling in narrow pleats, crossed at the hip by a band of black chain trim and ending in a wide black patterned hem. Dark gauntlets show at either side of it.
