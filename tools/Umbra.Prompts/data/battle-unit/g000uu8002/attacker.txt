Three-quarter front view of a towering figure built of bone and skulls. Human skulls with hollow eye sockets are packed into every surface between long conical spikes of pale bone. A rounded head of overlapping bone plates carries one bright yellow eye, a smooth oval set deep among the plates. Broad flat bone blades jut from the shoulders, and heavy arms of massed skulls and spikes hang to the ground. Thick legs of the same bone taper into broad spiked feet. The whole body is pale gray and tan bone, weathered and chipped, with dark hollows between the plates.
