Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in dark brown plate armor. A domed gray steel helm covers his head, and a thick dark brown beard spreads across his chest. A rounded steel breastplate sits at the center of the armor, and a dark red leather sleeve with gray bands runs down one arm to a dark red gauntlet. A pale leather belt crosses his waist above a dark brown studded skirt that hangs to his knees. Steel boots cover his feet. He holds a warhammer with a gray steel head on a long brown haft.
