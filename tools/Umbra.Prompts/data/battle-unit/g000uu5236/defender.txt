Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in dark brown plate armor. A domed gray steel helm covers the back of his head. A gray steel pauldron sits on one shoulder, and a gray banded sleeve runs down the arm below it. A black and white checkered kilt hangs from his waist to his knees, and steel boots cover his feet. He holds a warhammer with a gray steel head on a long brown haft, its haft crossing his back.
