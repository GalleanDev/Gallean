Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad-shouldered giant with pale blue-white skin. A spray of long white icicle spikes rises from the back of his head over a shaggy white mane. Dark blue-black plate armor of overlapping segments covers his back, with huge rounded pauldrons and a wide dark belt. Ragged white fur fringes hang from his shoulders and arms, and a shaggy white skirt falls from his hips to his knees. His thick bare arms end in broad pale hands, and he stands on huge bare feet. He holds a long curved sword with a ragged pale blue ice blade and a gold curling hilt.
