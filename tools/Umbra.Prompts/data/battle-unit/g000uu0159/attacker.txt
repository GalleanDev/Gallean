Three-quarter front view of a broad-shouldered giant with pale blue-white skin. A spray of long white icicle spikes rises from his head, and a long white beard of icicle strands falls from a ridged gray-blue brow and pale eyes. Dark blue-black plate armor, huge rounded pauldrons and a wide dark belt cover his chest. Ragged white fur fringes hang from his shoulders and knees over a skirt of dark blue plates. His thick bare arms end in broad pale hands, one in a dark studded bracer. He stands on huge bare feet. He holds a long curved sword with a ragged pale blue ice blade and a gold curling hilt.
