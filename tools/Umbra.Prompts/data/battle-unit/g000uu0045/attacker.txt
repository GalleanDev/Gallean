Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in brown leather and steel. He is a stern dwarf engineer. A brown scaled cap with gold trim covers the top of his head, with two coiled, striped horns curling up from it, and a pointed ear shows at its side. His face is clear and visible below the cap, and he wears small round brass-rimmed spectacles over his eyes, with bushy brows above them and weathered cheeks; a long pale tan mustache and beard begin below his nose and fall over his chest. Riveted steel plates and brown leather straps cover his shoulders and chest, and steel gauntlets cover his hands. A riveted steel drum rises behind his shoulder. A heavy pickaxe with a tan wooden haft and a dark gray curved head lies across his body. A wide leather belt holds a brown leather apron, and blue cloth with a gold edge shows at his legs above brown boots with steel toe caps.
