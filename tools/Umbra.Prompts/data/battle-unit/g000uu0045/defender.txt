Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in brown leather and steel. A brown scaled cap with gold trim covers the top of his head. A brown leather pack with gold scrollwork covers his back, with two gold-ringed cylinders, a curved golden rod and a riveted steel drum on it. A wide leather belt sits above blue cloth with a gold edge and brown boots with steel toe caps.
