Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in dark red armor. A soft red cap covers the back of his head, quilted into panels by pale cords, and pale blond hair falls beside it. Ribbed plates of dark red leather cover his back above a skirt of overlapping plates, with a brown belt at the waist. One arm reaches out and ends in a heavy studded gauntlet, and dark plated boots cover his feet. The pale bone-colored limbs of a crossbow show past his shoulder, and a bundle of bolts with pale blue-white fletching sits at his side.
