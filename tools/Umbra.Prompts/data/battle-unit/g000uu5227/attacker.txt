Three-quarter front view of a stout old dwarf in dark red armor. A wide soft red cap with a dark studded band sits low over his brow. Small round wire-rimmed spectacles rest on his broad weathered nose, the eyes behind them small and narrow, under thick bushy pale gold eyebrows. A huge beard of tight pale gold curls covers his cheeks, mouth and chin and spills down over his chest. Ribbed plates of dark red leather cover his torso above a skirt of overlapping plates, with tan trim at the shoulders, and dark plated boots cover his feet. He holds a crossbow level across his body, its pale bone-colored limbs curving forward to the right, and a bundle of bolts with pale blue-white fletching stands behind his shoulder.
