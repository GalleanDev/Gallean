Three-quarter front view of a tall winged demon with dark red hide over black bony plates. A crown of long black horns banded with pale gray sweeps back around its head, and it has small yellow eyes above an open muzzle of pale fangs. Ribbed plates cover its chest and shoulders, and its arms end in clawed black hands. Bat wings rise behind it, their deep red membranes stretched on black and pale gray striped bones hooked with spurs. It holds a long dark haft with a studded brown grip and a broad gray blade, and stands on heavy clawed feet.
