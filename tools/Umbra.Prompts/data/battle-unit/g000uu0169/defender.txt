Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall winged demon with dark red hide over black bony plates. A crown of long black horns banded with pale gray rises from the back of its head. Ridged plates run down its spine, and bat wings spread wide behind it, their bright red membranes stretched on black and pale gray striped bones hooked with spurs. A long ribbed tail hangs down to the ground. The dark haft of its weapon rises past its shoulder, wrapped in a studded brown grip, and it stands on heavy clawed feet.
