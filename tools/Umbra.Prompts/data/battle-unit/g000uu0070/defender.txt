Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged demon in black armor veined with red. The back of the head is a smooth dark mass, and pale gray curved horns sweep up from it. Pale gray blade-like spikes jut from the shoulders and hips. Large bat wings spread from the back, their dark red membranes carrying heavy red veins and braced by pale gray struts. Two long black strips hang from the belt to the ground behind the legs, which are cased in the same veined plating. A long black serrated blade reaches out past one shoulder.
