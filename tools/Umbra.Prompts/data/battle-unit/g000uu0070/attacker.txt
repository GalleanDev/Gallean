Three-quarter front view of a winged demon in black armor veined with red. A smooth black hood-like dome covers the head, and pale gray curved horns sweep up from it. Pale gray blade-like spikes jut from the shoulders and hips above a ribbed black abdomen. Large bat wings spread from the back, their black membranes edged with red and braced by pale gray struts. The arms and legs are cased in the same veined plating, ending in clawed feet with pale claws. A long black serrated blade is held across the body.
