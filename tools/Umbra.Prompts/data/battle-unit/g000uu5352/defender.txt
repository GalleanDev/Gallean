Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an old man buried in black feathers. A black feathered hood covers the back of his head. A heavy black feathered mantle spreads over his shoulders and upper arms in broad pointed lobes. Below it a black cloak with silvery vertical streaks falls in deep pleats to a pale cream hem near the ground. Brown leather sleeves end in brown leather gloves at each side.
