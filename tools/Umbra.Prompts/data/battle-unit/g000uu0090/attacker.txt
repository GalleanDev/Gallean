Three-quarter front view of a broad man in heavy dark armor. A black rounded helm sits over a gold-brown banded collar of lamellar plates that wraps the head and neck. Large black ribbed pauldrons cap the shoulders above a black plate cuirass, with gold-brown banded plates across the belly and a black ribbed belt of plates at the hips. A pleated copper-red skirt hangs from the belt to the shins, and copper-red cloth hangs behind the shoulders. Gold-brown banded guards cover the thighs and shins, one forearm carries a polished brass vambrace, and heavy brown boots cover the feet. He holds a long black spear with a barbed dark head.
