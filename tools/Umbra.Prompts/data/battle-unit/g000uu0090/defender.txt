Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad man in heavy dark armor. A long pale gray cloak with deep vertical folds falls from the shoulders and covers the back to the ground. Above it a black rounded helm sits over a gold-brown banded collar of lamellar plates, and large black ribbed pauldrons cap the shoulders.
