Three-quarter front view of a slender woman in a purple gown. A wide brass crown with two curving horns sits on her head, its band studded with bosses. Very long dark blue-black hair falls past her shoulders around a small face turned slightly down, half in the shadow of the crown, with pointed ears. Dark blue-gray plates cap her shoulders and a black torc circles her throat. The purple gown has a fitted bodice and bare arms, and it opens over a dark charcoal underskirt and a pale gray panel at the hip. Ribbed gray-brown leggings cover her legs to bare cream feet. She holds a long staff bound with gold bands and a black orb at one end.
