Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge hunched winged beast. Its orange-brown hide with heavy black stripes is ribbed and thickly muscled across the back and shoulders. A shaggy striped mane covers the back of its head, and a pale curved ridge shows above it. Tall dark brown leathery wings rise from its back, their ribbed membranes hanging in long folds and tipped with pale claws at the wing tips. Long arms ending in heavy black claws reach out to the side, a long striped tail curves down behind it, and it stands on broad feet with black claws.
