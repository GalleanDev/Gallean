Three-quarter front view of a huge hunched winged beast. Its orange-brown hide with heavy black stripes is ribbed and thickly muscled. Its head sits low between the shoulders in a shaggy striped mane, with two long pale ivory tusks curving down from it, small pale eyes and white fangs. Long arms ending in heavy black claws reach out from its shoulders, and it stands on broad feet with black claws. Tall dark brown leathery wings rise from its back, ribbed in long folds and tipped with pale claws at the wing tips, and a long striped tail trails behind it.
