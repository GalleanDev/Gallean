Three-quarter front view of a gaunt old man in a deep red robe, seen from above with his head bowed, so his pale forehead is the largest part of the face and the rest of it falls away below and to the right, strongly foreshortened. Heavy black hair hangs in a thick curtain down one side of his head and past his shoulders, and a dark red headband crosses the top of his forehead at the hairline. Under the brow his eyes are lost in shadow; only the ridge of the brow and the line of the nose read clearly, and his mouth is closed and still. A high red collar stands behind his neck, and a purple panel with gold stripes hangs down the front of the robe. Shoulder guards of jagged gray metal shards sit above dark sleeves, with a banded red bracer at one wrist and bare pale hands. A wide olive-gold sash belts the waist above an olive-gold skirt with a wide gold hem. A floor-length black cloak with gold trim hangs behind him over black boots.
