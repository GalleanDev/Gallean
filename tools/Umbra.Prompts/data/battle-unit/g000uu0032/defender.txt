Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge giant with pale teal skin. Two curved horns rise from the back of his head, one pale gold and one dark gray, above dark blue hair. A long fall of pale gray strands hangs down his back. A ruff of long pale blue-white spikes spreads over his shoulders and arms. A padded garment of pale gray wrappings covers his back under a brown leather belt. His thick arms end in teal hands with long claws, and spiky cuffs circle his wrists. A fringe of the same pale spikes hangs at his hips above thick legs and broad teal feet.
