Three-quarter front view of a huge giant with pale teal skin. Two curved horns rise from his head, one pale gold and one dark gray. A long stringy beard of dark blue strands hangs to his chest below a teal face with pale eyes. A ruff of long pale blue-white spikes spreads over his shoulders and down his arms. A padded garment of pale gray wrappings covers his chest and belly under a brown leather belt. His thick arms end in teal hands with long claws, and spiky cuffs circle his wrists. A fringe of the same pale spikes hangs at his hips above thick legs and broad teal feet.
