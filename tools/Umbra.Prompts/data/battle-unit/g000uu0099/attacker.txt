Three-quarter front view of a woman in a wide bell-shaped gown. Long white hair falls past her shoulders around a pale gray face with blank white eyes and dark lips. Her fitted bodice is cut low over a pale chest, and wide paneled upper sleeves sit above slim banded sleeves on her forearms. Dense dark lattice embroidery covers the upper sleeves and the whole skirt. The skirt spreads into a broad dome and opens down the front over a smooth pale underskirt, and a blush-pink band runs along the hem.
