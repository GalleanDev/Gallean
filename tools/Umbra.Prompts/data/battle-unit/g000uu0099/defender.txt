Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a woman in a wide bell-shaped gown. Long white hair falls in a heavy mass down the back of her head and over her shoulders. Her fitted bodice narrows to a waist above the skirt, and slim banded sleeves run down her arms. Dense dark lattice embroidery covers the whole skirt. From behind the skirt is an unbroken broad dome of pale gray reaching the ground, with a plain gray band above the hem and a blush-pink band along the very bottom.
