Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a serpent-bodied creature of dark bronze. A crown of curling bronze horns and thin prongs spreads around the back of its head. Bronze plates and a gray steel plate cover its shoulders, banded below with segmented bronze rings. Its body is a long ribbed bronze trunk that stands upright and curves down to the ground in a wide bend, with small barbs along its edges. From there a long tail trails away across the ground, fringed with more small barbs.
