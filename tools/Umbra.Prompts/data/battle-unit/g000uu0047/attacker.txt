Three-quarter front view of a broad dwarf in a long ribbed robe of olive and gold. He is a grim dwarf champion. A blue cap with a gold band and a gold ornament is pulled low over his brow, his eyes in shadow beneath it, and a thick pale cream beard covers the rest of his face and spreads across his chest. Black rounded pauldrons with pale steel edges and gold trim cap his shoulders above blue cloth with gold trim, and a copper band crosses his waist. A white cloth panel hangs down the front of the robe to the hem. Dark brown gnarled gloves cover his hands. He holds a dark gnarled staff set with a green orb.
