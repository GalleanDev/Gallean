Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad dwarf in a long ribbed robe. A blue cap with a gold band and a gold ornament sits on the back of his head, and a pale cream ruff shows at the collar. Black rounded pauldrons with pale steel edges and gold trim cap his shoulders above blue cloth with gold trim. The blue back of the robe falls in gold-striped folds to a gold hem. Dark brown gnarled gloves cover his hands. He holds a dark gnarled staff set with a green orb.
