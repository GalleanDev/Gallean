Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf under a broad, round brown hat pulled low, ringed below with shaggy gray hair, and a long gray beard covering his chest. His eyes are almost invisible, lost in the deep shadow under the rim of the hat, above a broad nose and the beard. Brown rolled shoulder guards sit on both shoulders over a cream tunic, gathered at the waist by a brown belt. A long dark brown staff with a white and blue ring at one end crosses his body below his big pale hands. Pale gray sandals cover his feet, with bare toes showing at the front.
