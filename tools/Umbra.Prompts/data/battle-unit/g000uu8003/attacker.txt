Three-quarter front view of a slender dark elf warrior woman with her head bent forward over a round shield. Her skin is a soft violet, the same lavender on her face, cheeks and neck as on her brow; her face is narrow with high cheekbones, a small straight nose and closed lips, her eyes lowered in shadow. Long pointed ears reach back through very long pale silver-blonde hair that falls forward over her shoulders to her knees. She wears dark gray segmented armor with white feathered fringes and bright blue cloth at the hips, banded gauntlets and tall segmented boots. The round shield has a black rim and pale radiating lines on its gray face. She holds a long narrow sword.
