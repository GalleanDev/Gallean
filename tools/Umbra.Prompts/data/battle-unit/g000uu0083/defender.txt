Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an armored man in black plate. A rounded black helm covers the back of the head, and a tall standing collar with gold edging and red lining rises on either side of it. Black pauldrons and black back plates are traced with fine gold lines. A dark brown cape with gold trim hangs from the shoulders to the ground in long straight folds, its hem banded in gold. Pale armored boots show at the bottom.
