Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf whose wild white hair spills between his shoulders. He wears brown leather armor studded with rivets, with a heavy brown pauldron on his raised arm and a brown gauntlet ending in steel claws. A heavy crossbow of pale wood and brass is slung high on his back, its pale stock, a dark hoop and a brown ribbed drum showing over one shoulder. A dark gray kilt hangs to his knees over heavy brown boots.
