Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf with wild white hair crossed by a brown leather strap with brass fittings. A large dark round lens over one eye sits above his tanned nose, and a thick white beard falls to his chest. He wears brown leather armor studded with rivets, with a heavy brown pauldron on his raised arm and a brown gauntlet ending in steel claws. He holds a heavy crossbow of pale wood and brass across his body, a flared golden bell at its front and a dark ribbed drum behind. A dark gray kilt hangs to his knees over heavy brown boots.
