Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a woman in pale bone armor. A tall dark brown miter stands over the back of her head, and long pale gray hair falls from under it down her back. Broad banded pauldrons of bronze and pale bone plates sit on her shoulders above bronze banded vambraces. A white cloak with a gold embroidered hem covers her from the shoulders to the ground, hanging in long folds that widen to a broad hem.
