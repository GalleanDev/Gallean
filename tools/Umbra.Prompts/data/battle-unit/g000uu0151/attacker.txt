Three-quarter front view of a woman in pale bone armor. A tall dark brown miter stands over her head, and long pale gray hair falls from under it to her shoulders around a small face turned slightly down, half in the shadow of the miter, with a closed mouth. Broad banded pauldrons of bronze and pale bone plates sit on her shoulders above bronze banded vambraces. A cream bodice laced with brown criss-cross cords covers her chest over a dark gray robe. A white cloak with a gold embroidered hem hangs at both sides. Pale bone thigh plates and greaves with bronze bands cover her legs above brown boots.
