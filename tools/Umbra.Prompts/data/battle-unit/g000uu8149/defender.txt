Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a dwarf seated inside a wheeled wooden war machine. A cage of rough dark timbers, braced and pinned with iron, rises around him on a low cart with thick wooden wheels. Tall gray metal tubes with open ends stand at one side, and an iron cog and a long metal rod sit at the other. Only the rounded rust-brown helmet shows above the frame, with a gray pipe bending away below it. Planks and diagonal braces close the whole back of the frame.
