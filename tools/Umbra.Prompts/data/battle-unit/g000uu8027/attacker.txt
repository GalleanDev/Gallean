Three-quarter front view of a broad man in white plate armor. Dark hair sweeps back above a pale face. Huge rounded pauldrons of ribbed white steel cap his shoulders over a ribbed white breastplate and banded plates across his belly. A dark red belt studded with gold and a round white boss holds a white layered tabard that hangs over his thighs. Ribbed white plate covers his legs down to white boots, and dark gauntlets cover his hands. A long cloak hangs from his shoulders, its black inner surface slashed with vertical gold streaks.
