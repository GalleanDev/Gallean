Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a heavy four-legged dragon. Its body is plated in dark gray-green armor with deep red flesh showing between the plates. Pale tan spikes stand along its back, shoulders, limbs and tail. The armored head sits at the end of a low neck, carrying pale tan horns and spines above a dark ridged snout that juts away from the body. Thick legs end in broad feet with long black claws. A long banded tail sweeps away behind it and ends in a hooked red tip.
