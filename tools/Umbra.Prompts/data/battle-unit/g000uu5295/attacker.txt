Three-quarter front view of a heavy four-legged dragon. Its body is plated in dark gray-green armor with deep red flesh showing between the plates. Pale tan spikes stand along its back, shoulders, limbs and tail. The head is low and armored, carrying pale tan horns and spines around a dark ridged snout and a dark red throat. Thick legs end in broad feet with long black claws. A long banded tail tapers away behind it and ends in a hooked red tip.
