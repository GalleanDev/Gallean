Three-quarter front view of a stocky man in brown leather armor, leaning forward. His head is bare, and long straight brown hair falls from a center parting past his shoulders, framing a lean, clean-shaven face; a brown hood lined with green and edged with a curved steel rim hangs pushed back behind his head. A heavy pale gray cloak is bunched over his shoulder and back, and a quiver of arrows rises behind it. He holds a long slender dark brown bow, and a dark chain hangs from his belt. Studded brown leather trousers meet wide polished steel bands at the knees, with white cloth wraps below them and heavy brown boots with pale toe caps.
