Three-quarter front view of a large tree that stands on its own roots. A thick trunk of deeply furrowed brown bark rises in vertical ridges, with a dark hollow cut into it high up. A broad crown of orange and olive-brown autumn leaves spreads across the top and hangs over the trunk. One limb of the same bark comes down at the side and ends in a knobbly wooden hand with short fingers. At the ground the trunk splits into wide splayed roots that spread out like feet, their worn wood pale where the bark has gone.
