Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large tree that stands on its own roots. A broad crown of orange and olive-brown autumn leaves fills most of the shape and reaches out in a long leafy limb to one side. Below it a thick trunk of deeply furrowed brown bark shows in vertical ridges, squat and pale where the bark has worn away, and a bare dark branch crosses it. At the ground the trunk splits into wide splayed roots that spread out like feet.
