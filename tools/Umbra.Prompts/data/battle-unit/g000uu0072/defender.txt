Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall winged demon in a long black robe streaked with gold. Thick curled gray horns show their outer coils at the back of the head, and a purple and gold spire rises between them. A purple and gold spiked collar of long thorns radiates from the shoulders. Purple plates veined with gold sit across the upper back above the robe, which falls in straight folds to the ground. Large bat wings spread wide, their dark gray membranes streaked with tan and carrying clawed spikes at the wing tips. Dark clawed feet show below the hem.
