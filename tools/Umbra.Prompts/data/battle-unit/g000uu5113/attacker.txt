Three-quarter front view of a hulking green-skinned orc, stooped forward. Shaggy black hair is gathered into a topknot with a red tie above a heavy green face with pointed ears and two short tusks jutting up from a closed mouth; his jaw is shut and his head is still. A deep red cuirass covers his chest and belly, with rounded deep red pauldrons and a segmented shoulder guard of overlapping gray plates. White cloth wrappings bind his green forearms above green clawed hands. A pale blue-gray cord crosses his chest and waist over deep red trousers, and white cloth wrappings circle his ankles above bare green clawed feet. He holds a short axe with a pale wooden haft and a dark iron head.
