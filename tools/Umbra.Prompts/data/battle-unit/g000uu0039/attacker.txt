Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in ornate bronze armor with swirling gold patterns. A dark bronze helm with a gold crest covers his head, carrying two dark horns banded with gold and white feathered wings at the sides of the helm. A long white beard falls from under the helm across his chest. A long pale gray striped cloak hangs over his shoulders, and heavy bronze boots cover his feet. He holds a broad pale gray blade in one hand and a dark square-headed maul on a brown haft in the other.
