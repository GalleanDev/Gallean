Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in ornate bronze armor with swirling gold patterns. A dark bronze helm with a gold crest covers the back of his head, carrying two dark horns banded with gold and white feathered wings at the sides of the helm. A long pale gray striped cloak covers his back and falls to the ground, where a gold-brown band runs along the hem. He holds a broad pale gray blade in one hand and a dark square-headed maul on a brown haft in the other.
