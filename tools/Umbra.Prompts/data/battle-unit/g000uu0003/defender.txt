Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an armored rider on a gray horse. A rounded steel helm sits low between his shoulders, and a rounded steel pauldron caps each shoulder beside it. A white mantle hangs from the lower rim of each pauldron: the cloth begins at the metal and falls unbroken from there down the rider's back, so the plates rest on his shoulders and are joined to what hangs below them. Between the pauldrons a narrow strip of his back shows, banded with gold and orange trim. He holds a long lance upright, its dark shaft rising well above him. The mantle runs into the white caparison edged with the same gold and orange trim, which covers the horse's rump and falls in pale vertical folds to its knees, with gray legs and dark hooves below. The horse's gray head turns aside, showing its dark ears and long muzzle.
