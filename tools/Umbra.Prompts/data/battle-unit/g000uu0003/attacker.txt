Three-quarter front view of an armored rider on a gray horse. He wears polished steel plate with a rounded steel helm, and a weathered face shows beneath its short peak. Large rounded pauldrons, fluted arm plates and steel gauntlets cover his arms above a steel skirt. He holds a long lance angled down past his leg. The horse carries a white caparison edged with gold and orange trim that hangs to its knees, leaving its gray head and gray legs bare. A brown leather bridle crosses its muzzle, and a brown strap runs over its chest.
