Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall figure in a bell-shaped robe. A broad pale bone crest with two long curved prongs spreads above the back of the head, and a pale column of bone plates rises to it from the shoulders. Dark teal cloth wraps the shoulders over pale bone plates bound with tan straps. Gray armor plates cap the hips above a wide skirt of black and deep red set with a row of large round gray discs and edged with a pale rim at the floor. A curved horn is fixed at one hip, a wide gray bell above a pale tapering shaft.
