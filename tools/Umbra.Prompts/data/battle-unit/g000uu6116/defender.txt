Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a heavy four-legged winged beast with reddish-brown fur. A thick shaggy mane of coarse fur covers the back of its head and spreads across its neck and shoulders. Two large bat-like wings of ribbed reddish-brown membrane rise from its back and open wide to either side, each carrying a hooked claw at its bend. Its long tail arches high over its body and ends in a swollen pale tip with a dark curved barb. Its haunches are heavy, and its broad hind paws carry heavy pale claws.
