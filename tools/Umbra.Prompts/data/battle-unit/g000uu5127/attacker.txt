Three-quarter front view of a huge octopus. Its mantle is a long pointed sac of mottled brown and tan, wrinkled with fine streaks, and a thin dark spine juts from its tip. Below the mantle a large dark eye sits under a heavy lid in a deeply furrowed head. Eight thick tentacles spread from the body across the ground, each ringed with darker brown bands and lined with pale suckers along the underside, tapering to thin whip-like tips. Two of them run far longer and thinner than the rest.
