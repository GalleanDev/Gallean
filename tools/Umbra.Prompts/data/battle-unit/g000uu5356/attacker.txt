Three-quarter front view of a broad man in heavy dark brown armor with a rough hammered texture. Swept-back white hair and a weathered face with a white mustache show small between huge rounded brown pauldrons. A cream beaded cord crosses his chest. A pale ram skull with long curling horns is set on the front of his armor, and he holds two long pale blue-gray swords. A long black pleated cloak with ornate cream and gold trim hangs from his shoulders down to his dark brown boots.
