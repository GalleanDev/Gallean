Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in black and white court dress. Two fans of black feathers rise behind his shoulders beside a white plume, and a smooth dark gray dome shows between them at the back of his head. A long cloak of pale gray pleats covers him from the shoulders to the ground, banded with two lines of ornate gold trim and edged with a wide gold hem. A tall dark staff topped with a dark four-pointed star and small gray wings stands above his shoulder.
