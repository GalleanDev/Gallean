Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a towering figure built of bone and skulls. Human skulls are packed into every surface between long conical spikes of pale bone. A rounded head of overlapping bone plates sits low between the shoulders, where broad flat bone blades jut out to the sides. Heavy arms of massed skulls and spikes hang to the ground. Thick legs of the same bone taper into broad spiked feet. The whole body is pale gray and tan bone, weathered and chipped, with dark hollows between the plates.
