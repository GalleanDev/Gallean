Three-quarter front view of a man in a deep red robe. He wears a wide-brimmed red hat with a tall square crown, a row of three tiny pale skull ornaments set along the edge of its brim, and a spray of pale gray spines at one side. Below the skull ornaments, in the shadow of the brim, shows a human man's face with ordinary tanned skin and dark eyes, and a red scarf is wrapped over his mouth and chin. A rounded steel cap sits on one shoulder and a rounded tan-brown cap on the other, each fringed with long pale bone spines. His robe falls in heavy folds striped in red and olive-tan, with a dark olive-brown sash across the chest and waist. He holds a long dark staff topped with a pale bone fork.
