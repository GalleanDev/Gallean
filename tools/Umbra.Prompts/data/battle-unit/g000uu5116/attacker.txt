Three-quarter front view of a hunched, heavily muscled troll with dark green skin mottled with pale yellow-green blotches. A shock of spiky black hair stands up from his head above a heavy green face with dark eyes under thick brows, pointed ears and a wide mouth of long pale fangs. His body is bare but for a red loincloth held by a brown leather belt at his hips. Long curved pale bone blades are bound to one forearm with brown bands, and his broad green hands end in long gray claws. He stands on splayed clawed green feet.
