Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hunched, heavily muscled troll with dark green skin mottled with pale yellow-green blotches. A shock of spiky black hair stands up from the back of his head, carried low between broad shoulders. His body is bare but for a red loincloth held by a brown leather belt at his hips. Long curved pale bone blades are bound to one forearm with brown bands, and his broad green hands end in long gray claws. He stands on splayed clawed green feet.
