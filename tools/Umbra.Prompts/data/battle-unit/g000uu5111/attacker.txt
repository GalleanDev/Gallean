Three-quarter front view of a centaur, an old man's torso on a dark brown horse body. Long silver-gray hair and a long gray beard frame a weathered pale face. Dark brown banded armor covers his chest and arms, with a tan star emblem at one shoulder and a wide black belt at his waist. Below it hangs a skirt of layered dark brown plates, and a black and white streaked caparison covers the horse body down to black hooves. He holds a long spear with a broad gold blade.
