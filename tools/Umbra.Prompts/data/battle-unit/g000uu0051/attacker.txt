Three-quarter front view of a broad, heavy figure carved from mottled gray-brown stone. A stone cap with an engraved band covers its head, and two large ribbed horns curl back beside a smaller pointed spike. An amber eye glints beneath the brim, above a long ribbed beard that falls across the chest. Thick slabs of stone form its chest, and a belt strap with a rounded studded ornament crosses the waist. A long cloak with dark panels and pale vertical stripes hangs from its shoulders to the ground. Its thick legs end in wide banded boots, and loose stone chunks hang in the air around it.
