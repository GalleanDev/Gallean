Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad, heavy figure carved from mottled gray-brown stone. The back of its head sits low between wide banded slabs of stone at the shoulders. A long cloak with dark panels and pale vertical stripes covers it from the shoulders to the ground and spreads wide across the whole back. Blocky stone hands show at its sides, and loose stone chunks hang in the air around it.
