Three-quarter front view of a tall muscular woman in a mottled dark brown leather jacket. A tall pale green-gray crest bound with blue bands rises from her head, and a blue cloth wraps her head above a stern, unpainted face with dark brows and a closed mouth. The jacket carries a round polished steel cup over one breast and a rounded pauldron on one shoulder. A deep blue cloth panel hangs from her neck to a dark studded belt and a short blue skirt. Her thighs are bare. Pale gray wrappings with dark straps cover her legs above strapped sandals, and a dark plated greave sits on one shin.
