Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in plate and leather. A tall pale cream helmet with a blue band covers the back of his head. A scaled shoulder guard of brown leather and dull steel sits over dark gray sleeves, with ribbed cream shoulder rolls. A long braided gray-brown mass hangs down the middle of his back, with dull steel plates and brown leather to either side above a brown leather belt. A pale cream quilted kilt hangs over pale cream leggings and red-brown boots with steel toe caps. He wears red-brown gloves and a round ringed metal flask, and holds a long pale horn with a flared metal-banded end.
