Three-quarter front view of a stout bearded dwarf in plate and leather. A tall pale cream helmet with a blue band sits over a broad nose, and a long braided gray-brown beard covers his chest. A polished steel breastplate sits under a coppery collar band. A scaled shoulder guard of brown leather and dull steel sits over dark gray sleeves, with ribbed cream shoulder rolls. A brown leather belt with a leather pouch cinches a pale cream quilted kilt above red-brown boots. He wears red-brown gloves, holds a long pale horn with a flared metal-banded end, and a round ringed metal flask hangs at his hip.
