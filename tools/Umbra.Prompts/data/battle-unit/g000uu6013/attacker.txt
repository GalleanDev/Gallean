Three-quarter front view of a standing figure wrapped head to foot in a deep red velvet robe. A tall pointed hood rises to a peak above the head, its leading edge banded with gold braid, and beneath it a red cloth and a small pale ornament cover the head. A short shoulder cape of the same red velvet falls over the shoulders, edged with gold braid, and a round gray medallion sits at its center. Below it a dark leather vestment crosses the chest above a gold cord. The long robe reaches the ground, with gold braid down its front opening and along its wide hem.
