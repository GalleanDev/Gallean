Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a standing figure wrapped head to foot in a deep red velvet robe. A tall pointed hood rises to a peak at the back of the head, tapering to a narrow tip. A short shoulder cape of the same red velvet covers the upper back, edged with gold braid along its lower rim. Below it the long robe falls to the ground in heavy vertical folds, a line of gold braid down its center and a wide band of gold braid along the hem.
