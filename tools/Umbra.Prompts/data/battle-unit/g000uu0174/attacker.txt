Three-quarter front view of a gaunt undead woman of ashen gray flesh. A thick veil of long white stringy hair hangs from the crown of her head down over her brow and cheeks to her chest, parting at the middle. In that gap is the face of a young woman long dead: fine feminine features, a narrow jaw and high cheekbones, a small closed mouth, hollow shadowed eyes, and gray skin drawn tight over the bone. Long ragged strips of dark red hang from its shoulders and spread outward in thin tatters, over a bare hollow chest and thin arms that end in blunt rounded tips. Its legs are long and thin, knobbled at the joints, and taper to narrow bony feet.
