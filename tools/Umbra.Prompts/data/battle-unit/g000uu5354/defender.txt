Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in dark purple armor. Long white hair covers the back of his head and falls in strands to his shoulders. A cage of gold ribs curves over the purple back plate below rounded pauldrons rimmed with gold. A long black cape falls behind him to the ground in heavy folds. A gold-trimmed skirt of purple plates hangs from his belt above purple leg plates and brown boots, and a long pale gray strip hangs from the belt to the knee.
