Three-quarter front view of a man in dark purple armor. Long white hair falls in strands over his shoulders and chest, and a purple cloth mask covers the lower part of his face. Rounded pauldrons rimmed with gold cap his arms above a segmented purple cuirass banded with gold. A gold-trimmed skirt of purple plates hangs from his belt, with gold crescents at the hip, and purple leg plates run down to brown boots. A long black cape falls behind him to the ground. He holds a long slender straight sword, and a long pale gray strip hangs from the belt to the knee.
