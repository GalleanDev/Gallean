Three-quarter front view of a griffin with a black feathered head and body. A golden hooked beak curves from its black head, and its eye is golden. Glossy black feathers cover its neck, chest and long raised wings. Tan leather harness straps cross its shoulders and carry a gold plate. Its hindquarters are dark brown swirled with tan scrollwork, and shaggy cream feathering covers its legs above black claws. A brown tail ends in a broad paddle of dark fur.
