Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large bear on all fours, low and heavy-set. Its thick cream white fur is dense and slightly matted, with pale tan patches across the shoulders and haunches and faint rust-colored staining near the joints. The broad rump fills the view, with a short pale tail set high on it. A heavy shoulder hump rises ahead of the rump, and the lowered head shows only the back of the skull and one small rounded ear past it. The thick legs end in wide paws with short black claws.
