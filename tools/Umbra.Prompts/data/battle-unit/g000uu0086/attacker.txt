Three-quarter front view of a lean man in dark brown leather armor. He wears no helmet. His bald head and face are covered in raw reddish-brown scarred skin, stitched together in crude seams across the scalp and cheeks like old leather, with sunken eyes and a lipless grim mouth, above a wide tan leather gorget. A large rounded pauldron of dark brown leather sits on one shoulder, and red sleeves streaked with dark stripes cover the arms above tan leather bracers with gold edging. A red skirt streaked with the same dark stripes hangs to mid-thigh over dark brown leg armor and brown boots. He holds a sword low in his left hand, on the left side of the image, by its dark grip, with a small gold pommel and a single small gold crossguard next to that hand. The blade is one long, straight, plain gray steel blade that tapers to a point; nothing else is on it. His right hand, on the right side of the image, is bare skin, not armor: a long open hand that reaches down past the sword, palm and all fingers below the blade, fingertips pointing down.
