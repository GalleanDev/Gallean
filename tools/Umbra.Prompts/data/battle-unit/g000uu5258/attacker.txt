Three-quarter front view of a huge muscular winged demon. Its body is a dark red muscled hide, glossy and deeply ribbed. Large ribbed gray horns curve up and back from its head, above a red face with bright yellow eyes and a wide mouth of white fangs. Gray bone spikes stud its shoulders, elbows and forearms, above dark bands across the chest and belly. Its hands end in heavy gray claws and it stands on broad clawed feet. Large leathery wings of brown mottled membrane open from its back, hooked with gray spurs along their edges, and a long thick tail lined with gray bony spikes curves behind it.
