Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge muscular winged demon. Its dark red muscled hide is glossy and deeply ribbed, humped with muscle across the shoulders and spine. Large ribbed gray horns curve up and back from the back of its head. Gray bone spikes stud its shoulders and arms, and more run down its spine. Large leathery wings spread wide from its back, their deep red outer membrane veined and hooked with gray spurs along the edges. A long thick tail lined with gray bony spikes curls out to the side, and it stands on broad clawed feet.
