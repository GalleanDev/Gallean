Three-quarter front view of a lean man in a teal tunic. Shaggy dark brown hair covers his head, with a pale curved feather above it and a long pointed pale ear at the side. A dark fur mantle with thin black quills sits over his shoulders under a large rounded brown leather pauldron. A high teal collar wraps his throat. Brown leather bracers cover his forearms, and mottled gray scale armor covers his thighs. A wide dark brown belt holds a teal skirt above gray greaves and brown boots with tan toecaps. He holds a dark brown recurve bow, with pale arrow shafts behind him.
