Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean man in a teal tunic. Shaggy dark brown hair covers the back of his head, with a pale curved feather above it and a long pointed pale ear. A dark fur mantle with thin black quills covers his back under a large rounded brown leather pauldron. A high teal collar wraps his neck. Brown leather bracers cover his forearms, and a teal skirt hangs from a wide dark brown belt over mottled gray scale armor on his thighs. He wears brown boots with tan toecaps. A dark brown recurve bow and pale arrow shafts show past his side.
