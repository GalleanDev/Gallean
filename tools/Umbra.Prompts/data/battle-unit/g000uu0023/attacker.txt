Three-quarter front view of a man wrapped head to foot in a deep blue hooded cloak. The hood is up, its opening turned aside and dark, with a worn brown patch on the crown and a gray braid along the edge. More brown patches mark the cloak, which falls in heavy folds from the shoulders to the ground and hides the arms and body. A slender pale blade shows at the edge of the cloak, and the toe of a brown boot juts below the hem.
