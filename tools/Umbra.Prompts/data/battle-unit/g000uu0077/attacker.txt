Three-quarter front view of a tall armored figure in dark brown plate covered with raised gold scrollwork. A deep red pointed hood rimmed with gold covers the head, its opening a black hollow. Broad rounded pauldrons sit above a breastplate worked with the same scrollwork, and long armored tassets hang over armored greaves and heavy boots. Dark clawed gauntlets cover the hands. A deep red cloak falls from the shoulders past one side to a gold hem at the ground. It holds a long straight sword with a pale steel blade and a ridged edge.
