Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in a pale gray hood and cloak, the hood pulled low so its opening is dark. Only a broad tan nose and a dark brown beard show below that shadow; no eyes, brows or forehead are visible, and the head is still. Dark steel scale plates studded with pale gray rivets cover his shoulders and upper arms, and a dark red cloak hangs behind him. A pale gray tunic falls to his knees, crossed by brown leather belts, with a pale gray pouch at the front. A long pale cloth strip hangs at his side. Dark brown boots show below the hem. He holds a slender straight dagger in each hand.
