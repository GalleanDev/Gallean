Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking beast with gray stone-like hide mottled with green moss. Dark red vines twist over its limbs and cross its broad back, and more sprout from the back of its head like bare branches. Two blunt horns rise from the head. Heavy gray plates cover the shoulders and hips, and long arms end in clawed hands. Small bones dangling on thin strings hang at the hips. Thick legs stand on broad clawed feet.
