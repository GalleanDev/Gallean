Three-quarter front view of a hulking beast with gray stone-like hide mottled with green moss. Dark red vines twist over its limbs and chest and sprout from its head like bare branches. Its long gray muzzle carries a row of jagged teeth. Heavy gray plates cover the shoulders, and long arms end in clawed hands. A pale human skull hangs at each hip from a cord, with small bones dangling on thin strings beside it. Thick legs stand on broad clawed feet.
