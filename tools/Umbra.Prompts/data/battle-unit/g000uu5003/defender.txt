Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in banded bronze plate. A shaggy mane of pale gray hair covers the back of the head under a bronze helmet band. Overlapping bronze lames cross the back over a dark gray underlayer, and a broad brown leather belt cinches the waist above layered tassets at the hips. A segmented bronze vambrace covers the forearm above a brown leather glove. Red and white striped hose wrap one shin and a ribbed brown legging the other, both above heavy brown boots. He holds a long spear with a gold-banded shaft and a pale steel head on a crossbar.
