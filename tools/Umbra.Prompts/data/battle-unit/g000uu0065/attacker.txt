Three-quarter front view of a winged man with deep red skin. Black hair tops his head, and pale gray horns curve up from it. His face is broad and deep red, with pale yellow eyes. His chest is bare and muscled, and deep red banded plates cover his upper arms, waist and legs, with gold-yellow patches at the shoulders, hips and thighs. His bare red hands and feet end in short claws. Large bat wings open behind him, one membrane pale cream and the other near-black, both edged in red, and a long red tail tapers down behind him.
