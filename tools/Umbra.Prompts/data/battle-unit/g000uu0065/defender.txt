Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged man with deep red skin. Long black hair falls down his back from the back of his head, and pale gray horns curve up above it. Deep red banded plates cover his upper arms, waist and legs, with gold-yellow patches at the shoulders, hips and thighs. His bare red hands and feet end in short claws. Large bat wings spread behind him, one membrane pale cream and the other near-black, both edged in red, and a long red tail tapers away behind him.
