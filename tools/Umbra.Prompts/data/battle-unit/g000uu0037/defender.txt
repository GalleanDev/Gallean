Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in gray scale armor. A rounded steel helm with two curved golden horns sits on the back of his head, bound by a red-brown leather band. A long gray scale coat with brown straps covers his back to the knees, with white frilled trim at the shoulders and cuffs and a long white panel hanging at the back. Reddish-brown leather gauntlets cover his hands, and a brown leather belt crosses his waist. Bare pale legs show above brown boots with steel caps. He holds a warhammer with a black and steel head on a long brown haft.
