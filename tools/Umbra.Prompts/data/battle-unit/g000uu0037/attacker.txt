Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in gray scale armor. A rounded steel helm with two curved golden horns covers his head, bound by a red-brown leather band that sits low over his brow. Under it shows a broad, lined, weathered face with bushy dark brown brows shading small dark eyes, a broad nose and weathered cheeks above a thick dark brown beard that spreads over his chest, his expression stern and steady. A long gray scale coat with brown straps reaches his knees, with white frilled trim at the shoulders and cuffs, and a long white panel hangs down one side. Reddish-brown leather gauntlets cover his hands, and a brown leather belt crosses his waist. He holds a warhammer with a black and steel head on a long brown haft, and brown boots with steel caps cover his feet. When he spins to swing the hammer his back turns toward the viewer, showing only the smooth rounded back of the steel helm, the leather band and the scale coat across his broad shoulders.
