Three-quarter front view of a centaur with a bronzed torso on a pale gray horse body. A pale cream cap bound with a studded brown leather band covers his head, and a cream cloth wraps his neck and chin below a weathered face. A broad cream sash crosses his bare chest, and a long cream cloth hangs from his waist over the horse's shoulders. Brown leather armbands ring his upper arms above shaggy dark brown fur gauntlets.
