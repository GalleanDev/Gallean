Three-quarter front view of a heavy four-legged winged beast with reddish-brown fur. A thick shaggy mane of coarse fur covers its head, neck and shoulders, and within it sits a broad flat face with a pale muzzle, a dark brow and bared square teeth. Two large bat-like wings of ribbed reddish-brown membrane rise from its back. Its long tail arches high over its body and ends in a swollen pale tip with a dark curved barb. Four thick legs stand on broad paws with heavy pale claws, and the hide beneath the mane is smooth.
