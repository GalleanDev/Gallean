Three-quarter front view of a winged man in dark steel plate armor. Long dark hair falls beside a young, stern face with smooth gray skin and a closed mouth, and a large round stone disc engraved with concentric rings stands behind his head. Layered shoulder and arm plates cover his arms, and the armor is ridged and weathered like carved stone. Huge gray-white feathered wings spread from his back, their long feathers trailing into thin points. Armored legs with knee plates end in heavy boots, and he holds a long slender lance across his body.
