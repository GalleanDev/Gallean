Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a winged man in dark steel plate armor. A large round stone disc engraved with concentric rings covers the back of his head. Huge gray-white feathered wings spread wide from his back, their long feathers trailing into thin points. Layered shoulder and arm plates cover his arms, and the armor is ridged and weathered like carved stone, with a smooth pale plate down the back. Armored legs with knee plates end in heavy boots.
