Three-quarter front view of a broad man in blue-gray steel plate armor. Black hair swept back over long pointed ears frames his pale, clean-shaven face with gray eyes. Layered blue-gray pauldrons cover his shoulders above a breastplate set with pale round bosses, and dark gauntlets cover his hands. A long blue cloak with a white panel down the center and a silver-patterned hem band hangs from his shoulders to his ankles. Armored greaves and boots show below it. He holds two long thin swords.
