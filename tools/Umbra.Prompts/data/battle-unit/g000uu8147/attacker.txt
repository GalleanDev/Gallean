Three-quarter front view of a huge hulking giant with mottled purple-gray skin. A crown of pale bone lumps rings his broad head, and a single narrow eye sits above a wide mouth of brown teeth. Tan leather bands wrap his thick arms, and shaggy gray-white fur edges his shoulders and hips. A cord across his chest carries a pale skull, a pale hand and bone beads. A wide tan hide wrap covers his hips above bare purple legs and broad bare feet. One raised hand grips a gray boulder, and a second gray boulder rests on the ground beside his feet.
