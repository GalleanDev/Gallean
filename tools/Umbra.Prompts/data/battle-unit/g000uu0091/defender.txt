Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall man in black plate armor. A cloak of dark purple with a wide gold-brown trimmed border falls from the shoulders and pools on the ground behind him. Two long pale bone horns curve up from a black helm above huge black ribbed pauldrons. One arm is a silvery mail sleeve ending in a clawed steel gauntlet. It holds a long sword with an ornate gold hilt set with a red stone and a pale gray blade with a serrated back edge, its long grip ending in a small pointed blade.
