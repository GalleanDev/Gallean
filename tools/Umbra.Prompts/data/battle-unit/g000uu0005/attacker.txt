Three-quarter front view of an older man in black and steel armor under a long red cape. A deep red hat with a folded brim sits on his head, with a spray of pale gray feathers rising behind it. Long straight gray hair falls from under the brim to his shoulders around a lean, lined, weathered face with gray stubble on the jaw, narrow dark eyes, a long straight nose and thin closed lips, set in a stern, tired expression. A pale gray scarf is wrapped around his throat. A steel collar and a broad steel gorget cover his chest above a black banded cuirass and a black skirt with bronze bands. Panels of orange and brown striped cloth hang at his hips. Steel vambraces and gauntlets cover his arms, and steel greaves meet gray boots. He holds a long wooden mace with a dark metal head.
