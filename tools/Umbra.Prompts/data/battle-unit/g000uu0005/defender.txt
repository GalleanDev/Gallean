Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in black and steel armor under a long red cape. A deep red hat with a folded brim covers the back of his head, with a spray of pale gray feathers rising behind it. Steel collar plates sit at his neck above the cape, which falls in deep pleats from his shoulders to the ground, edged and lined with thin gold cord. Steel vambraces and gauntlets cover his arms, and gray boots show below the hem. He holds a long wooden mace with a dark metal head.
