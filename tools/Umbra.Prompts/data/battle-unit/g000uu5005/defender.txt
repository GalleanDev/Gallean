Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an elf in a long pale gray cloak. Pale mint-green hair covers the back of his head. Rounded brass pauldrons with dark studded rims sit above pale gray armored sleeves and gauntlets. The cloak is swept open, showing a lining striped in rust orange and dark brown, and ends in a wide dark green hem band patterned with white dashes. A pale tan panel and a dark belt cross the small of his back, and dark green cloth hangs over pale gray plate legs and boots. He holds a slender gold staff banded with dark rings and a small curled hook.
