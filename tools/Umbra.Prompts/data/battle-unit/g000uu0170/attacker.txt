Three-quarter front view of a crouching purple demon with ribbed hide streaked in pale violet. Dark horns curve back from its head, which has yellow eyes and long white teeth, and a smaller fanged head sits on its lower leg. Heavy shoulders and arms of the same ribbed hide end in long clawed fingers. Bat wings rise behind it, their brown membranes stretched on purple struts tipped with black hooked spurs. It holds a long dark shaft that runs down past its side, and it stands on broad clawed feet.
