Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender woman in a long black gown. A crown of long curved horns banded in black and tan sweeps up from her head, with pale bone ornaments at their bases and black hair swept into a crest between them. Two small blue points sit in the black hair. A large white fan of openwork tracery rises beside her shoulder. A dark red sash and pale bone chains wrap the black bodice. The gown flares to the floor, showing a deep red underskirt at the hem and rows of round silver-white rosettes and dark openwork panels down its sides.
