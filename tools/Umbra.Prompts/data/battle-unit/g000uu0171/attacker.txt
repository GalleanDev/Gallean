Three-quarter front view of a lean winged humanoid in banded armor of dark brown and polished steel. Its smooth pale head is domed and carries a low brown crest, and a small amber eye shows beneath it. Plated bands wrap its arms and legs, with steel caps at the knees and pointed steel boots. Broad bat wings spread from its shoulders, their olive-gray membranes stretched on bright silver ribs with small hooked claws at the tips. A segmented dark brown tail hangs down between its legs. It stands bent forward with its arms drawn in across its chest.
