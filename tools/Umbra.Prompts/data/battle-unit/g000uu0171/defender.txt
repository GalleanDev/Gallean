Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean winged humanoid in banded armor of dark brown and polished steel. Its smooth pale head sits low between steel shoulder caps, and a ridged column of dark brown segments runs down its spine. Plated bands wrap its arms and legs, with steel caps at the knees and pointed steel boots. Broad bat wings spread wide from its shoulders, their dark gray membranes stretched on bright silver ribs with small hooked claws at the tips. A segmented dark brown tail hangs down between its legs and curves to the ground.
