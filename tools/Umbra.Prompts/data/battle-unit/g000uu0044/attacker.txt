Three-quarter front view of a heavy dwarf guard. His head is a closed black iron helmet with two long dark curved horns sweeping up and outward; its visor is a solid plate of black iron from brow to beard, showing no skin. From under the visor a huge black bristling beard spills down over his chest, flanked by two tufts of cream fur at the collar. Black fur mantles bulk over his shoulders, a red cloak with gold edging hangs behind, and a broad gold belt plate sits over a dark brown coat. He grips a long-hafted axe with a wide dark curved blade.
