Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender elf woman in a long gray-green robe. A crown of gray thorny twigs studded with small red berries rings the back of her head. A long dark red cloak falls from her shoulders in deep vertical folds and reaches the ground, and the gray-green robe shows along its edges. Low at one side, a broad hem of gray bark hung with red and orange leaves widens out from under the cloak.
