Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in gray steel plate armor with gold-brown banding, his head bowed so he looks down at the ground. At the center of the picture his small weathered face shows clearly between long golden-brown hair and a great golden-brown beard that spills across his chest. Rounded steel pauldrons cover his shoulders above pale gloves with a yellow cuff. A dark green kilt hangs from his waist, and gray steel boots cover his feet. He holds a warhammer with a gray stone head on a long brown haft with a gold band.
