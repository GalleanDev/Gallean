Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged beast whose whole body is built of ropy red muscle strands. Three wolf-like heads crowd together on thick necks at the front of a heavy hunched body, each with a long snout, pale fangs and small yellow eyes. A mane of matted dark red strands falls over the heads, the neck and the shoulders. The forelegs are thick and sinewy and end in broad paws with thick toes and dark claws. A long tapering tail of the same red cords curves out behind the haunches.
