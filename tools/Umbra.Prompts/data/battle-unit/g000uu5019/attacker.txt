Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged dragon with deep red scales banded with darker red. Long backswept pale ivory horns rise from its head above a yellow eye, a narrow red snout and a jaw of white teeth. A pale gray ribbed underside runs down its long neck to its belly. Large bat wings with gray-brown membranes spread from its shoulders, and a row of small pale spines runs along its back. Four heavy legs end in pale ivory claws, and a long thick banded tail curls out behind it.
