Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged dragon with deep red scales banded with darker red. Long backswept pale ivory horns rise from its head, turned aside to show a narrow red snout in profile. Large bat wings with deep red membranes ribbed in darker red spread wide from its shoulders, and a row of small pale spines runs along its back. Four heavy legs end in pale ivory claws, and a long thick banded tail curls out behind it.
