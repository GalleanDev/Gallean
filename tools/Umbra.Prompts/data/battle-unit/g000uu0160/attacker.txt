Three-quarter front view of a short woman whose head is enclosed in a helm of woven brown branches and pale slats, wrapped in green vines with small leaves. Under the helm a white cloth wraps across the left side of her face, and only a sliver of her right cheek shows, a woman of middle age. A white cloak with a pointed hem covers her shoulders over a pale gray tunic with dark green panels. Rough mottled tan-brown shoulder pieces cap her arms, which end in bare tan hands, and a gnarled dark brown branch with small green sprigs curves up from her shoulder. Pale gray leggings with dark green stripes cover her legs, and copper bands circle her ankles above heavy brown boots.
