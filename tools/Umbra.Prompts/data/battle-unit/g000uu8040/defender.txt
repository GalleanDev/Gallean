Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender elf woman in dark segmented armor. Very long pale blonde hair with dark tips falls down her back past the knees from a smooth pale lavender crest over the crown. White feathered fringes hang at the shoulders above bright blue upper sleeves, and dark gray banded gauntlets cover her forearms. Blue plates sit at the hips over a tan cloth wrap, and tall dark gray segmented boots reach her thighs. A large round shield with a black rim rides behind the far shoulder, dark on this side. She holds a long slender sword with a narrow blade.
