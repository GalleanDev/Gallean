Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hulking giant built from stacked slabs of brown and gray stone. Its back is a rounded hump of layered rock, with fine lines fanning out from the top and two large ovals carved into it. Two boulder-like masses sit high on the shoulders, and the hump rises between them. Long thick arms hang down, ending in blocky hands with short blunt fingers. Broad bands of carved stone circle its wrists, hips and ankles, incised with spirals and rings. Squat columnar legs stand on broad flat feet.
