Three-quarter front view of a hulking giant built from stacked slabs of brown and gray stone. Two boulder-like masses rise above its small head, which sits low between them under a heavy carved slab. A pale blue eye sits in the carved stone face. Broad bands of carved stone circle its wrists, hips and ankles, incised with spirals and rings. Its arms are long and thick, ending in blocky hands with short blunt fingers. Layered rock plates cover the barrel chest. Squat columnar legs stand on broad flat feet with stubby toes.
