Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender woman in a purple gown. A wide brass crown with two curving horns sits on her head, its band studded with bosses. Very long dark blue-black hair falls in heavy strands down her back to the waist. Dark blue-gray plates cap her shoulders. The purple gown falls from a fitted bodice in wide vertical folds to the ground, and her bare arms show at the sides. She holds a long staff bound with gold bands.
