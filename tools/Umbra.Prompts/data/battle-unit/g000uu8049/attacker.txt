Three-quarter front view of a dwarf seated inside a wheeled wooden war machine. A cage of rough dark timbers, braced and pinned with iron, rises around him on a low cart with thick wooden wheels. Tall gray metal tubes with open ends stand at one side, and an iron cog and a long metal rod sit at the other. The figure wears a rounded rust-brown helmet and gray plates over its shoulders, with its dark gray gauntlets held in front of it. A gray mechanism of gears and cylinders sits low inside the frame. He is a bearded dwarf engineer inside the armor of the machine. Small round black engineering goggles with dark leather straps sit over his eyes under the helmet rim, and a thick bushy beard covers the lower half of his face, so almost nothing of his skin shows between the goggles and the beard. A small red stone sits low on the frame.
