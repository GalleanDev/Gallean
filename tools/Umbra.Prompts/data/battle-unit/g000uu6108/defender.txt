Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a broad, heavy-set man in black and gold plate armor. A pale bone-colored domed cap covers the back of his head above a black mail collar. Wide rounded pauldrons of black horizontal ribs cover his shoulders, and olive-gold bands run down the armor of one arm to a gauntlet. A long dark purple cape falls in deep vertical folds to the ground and hides the rest of him. He holds a long orange-brown wooden haft with a broad dark gray pointed blade.
