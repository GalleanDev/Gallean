Three-quarter front view of a broad, heavy-set man in black and gold plate armor. A pale bone-colored domed cap with a dark ornamented band sits on his head above a wide pale face and long dark hair. Wide rounded pauldrons of black horizontal ribs cover his shoulders, and a round breastplate with a domed center boss sits above a broad belt with a pale curved band. Olive-gold panels hang from his waist over dark greaves and heavy dark boots. A dark purple cape hangs behind him. He holds a long orange-brown wooden haft with a broad dark gray pointed blade.
