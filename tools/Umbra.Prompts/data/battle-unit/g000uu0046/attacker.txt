Three-quarter front view of an old stout dwarf in a dark red-brown robe, broad across the shoulders with a small head sitting low between them. Shaggy white hair and a great white beard frame that small head, and only the upper half of the face shows between them: a narrow band of weathered brow and deep-set eyes in shadow, with no spectacles or eyeglasses of any kind. Everything below that band is beard, which covers the nose, the mouth and the chin completely and spreads down over his chest. The robe falls in heavy vertical folds streaked with rust red, and a pale gray ribbed panel hangs down its front. Brown gloves cover his hands, which hold a long pale horn banded with metal that curves down to a wide flared bell. Dark brown boots show below the hem.
