Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a stout dwarf in a dark red-brown robe. White shaggy hair covers the back of his head and spreads over his shoulders. The robe falls in heavy vertical folds streaked with rust red and is tied at the waist with a dark braided cord, and a pale band runs along its hem. A long pale horn banded with metal reaches past his shoulder to a wide flared bell. Dark brown boots show below the hem.
