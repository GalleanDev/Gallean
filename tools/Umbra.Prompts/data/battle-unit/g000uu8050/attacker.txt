Three-quarter front view of a broad-shouldered man in blue-gray plate armor. A closed blue steel helm with gold scrollwork covers his head, pierced by a narrow dark slit, and two fans of golden spikes flare from its sides. Rounded pauldrons sit above a molded breastplate with brown-gold engraving and two wide dark brown leather belts. A ragged pale gray cloth hangs from the belt between his thighs, and a brown cloak lining shows behind his hips. His legs are plated to the dark brown boots, and a dark gauntlet hangs open at his side. He holds a broad steel sword with a golden hilt and crossguard.
