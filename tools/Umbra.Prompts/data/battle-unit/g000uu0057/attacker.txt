Three-quarter front view of a massive muscular winged demon with olive-gold hide banded in dark stripes. Its heavy head sits low between huge shoulders, where two large dark ribbed horns curve down around it in a ring. Small pale spikes stand on its crown, its yellow eyes sit above a wide mouth of white fangs, and two pale tusks curve up from its jaw. Pale gray spikes jut from one shoulder. Two large brown membranous wings open above its back, edged with small spurs. A thick banded tail sweeps out behind it, and its heavy limbs end in broad clawed feet.
