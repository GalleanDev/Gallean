Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a massive muscular winged demon with olive-gold hide banded in dark stripes. The back of its heavy head sits low between huge shoulders, ridged and set with small pale spikes, and one dark ribbed horn shows at its side. Two large brown membranous wings open wide from its back, edged with small spurs. A ridged spine runs down its broad back. A thick banded tail curves down and trails along the ground, and its heavy limbs end in broad clawed feet.
