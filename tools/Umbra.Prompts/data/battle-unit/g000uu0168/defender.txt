Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge hunched demon armored in glossy black chitin. Overlapping plates banded with pale gray cover its back, ridged and knobbed along the spine. Two broad wings of dull tan membrane spread wide on ribbed black struts with spiked tips. Long ribbed feelers arc out from its shoulders, and one of its limbs ends in a pale bone-white claw at its side. It stands on thick banded legs with clawed feet, and a long banded tail hangs down and curls along the ground.
