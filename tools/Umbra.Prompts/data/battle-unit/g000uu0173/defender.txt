Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean red-skinned woman with mottled red hide. Long black hair falls down her back, and two pale tan horns, ridged and curling, spread from the top of her head. Her back is bare, and black bands wrap her upper arms and forearms. A dark red sash sits at her hips over long red leggings banded with black at the knees. Wings of rust-brown membrane spread wide on red-brown ribs, and a long red tail hangs down and curls to the ground.
