Three-quarter front view of a tall figure in a long black hooded robe. Under the hood is the small head of a gaunt man, narrow and tapering to a pointed chin, with hollow dark eyes and a dark closed mouth, framed by black hair. The robe falls in heavy folds from broad shoulders, and small white bone ornaments hang down the front of it. A gold sash crosses the waist. Her pale forearms are wrapped in black and white bands, and her long pale fingers reach past the sleeves. A pale gray panel of the robe shows between the folds and falls to a gold-trimmed hem at the ground.
