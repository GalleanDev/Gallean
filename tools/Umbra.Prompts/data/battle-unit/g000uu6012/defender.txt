Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a huge giant with reddish-brown skin, holding a massive wooden club studded with gray stones out to one side. A shaggy black fur mantle with red cords woven through it lies across his shoulders, hung with dark teardrop pendants on gray beads. A pale bone dome shows at the top of his head, and a bone-white horned skull with thin white spikes sits at one shoulder. A wide dark belt studded with pale rivets crosses his back above an olive-brown cloth kilt. A red banded cloth is tied at one wrist, and his feet are bare and broad.
