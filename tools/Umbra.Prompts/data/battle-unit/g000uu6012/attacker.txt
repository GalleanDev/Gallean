Three-quarter front view of a huge giant with reddish-brown skin, bent forward over a massive wooden club studded with gray stones. A shaggy black fur mantle with red cords woven through it covers his shoulders and chest. A bone-white skull with a dark eye socket and a long curved tusk sits on his head, and a larger horned beast skull with pale spikes hangs below it. An olive-brown cloth kilt is wrapped at his waist above bare legs, and a red banded cloth is tied at one wrist. His feet are bare and broad.
