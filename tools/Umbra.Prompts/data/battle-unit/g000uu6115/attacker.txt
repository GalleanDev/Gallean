Three-quarter front view of a short thickset dwarf in smooth dark green armor with a heavy rounded belly. A long bushy silver-gray beard spills from under his broad tan nose and spreads across his chest. A rounded green cap sits low over his head above a pale metal band and deep-set eyes. A brown hide cloak hangs from one shoulder down his side. A pale cord belt crosses below the belly above a black studded kilt, and his legs and broad feet are green. A war hammer rests against his shoulder, its thick green head ringed with raised bands on a long brown wooden handle.
