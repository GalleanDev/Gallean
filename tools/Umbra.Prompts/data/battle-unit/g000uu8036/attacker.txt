Three-quarter front view of a small tree that stands on its own roots. A slender trunk of mottled gray-brown bark rises in ridges and knots and forks into thin bare branches. A dense rounded crown of orange and olive-brown autumn leaves covers the upper part and hangs down at the sides. Near the ground the trunk divides into thick roots that splay outward across the earth like feet, their pale wood scarred and worn. Patches of green moss cling to the lower bark.
