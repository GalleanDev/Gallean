Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall armored figure wrapped in a deep red cloak. A deep red pointed hood rimmed with gold covers the back of the head. The cloak falls in long straight folds to a gold hem at the ground. Broad rounded pauldrons of dark brown plate covered with raised gold scrollwork rise above it on both sides, and a dark clawed gauntlet shows at the end of one arm. Heavy boots stand below the hem, and a long straight sword with a pale steel blade and a ridged edge angles out to the side.
