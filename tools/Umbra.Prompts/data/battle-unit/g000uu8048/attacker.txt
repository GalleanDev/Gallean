Three-quarter front view of a lean mauve-skinned goblin. A pale bone skull with dark sockets shows through a heavy mane of black hair. A brown leather pauldron with tan trim caps one shoulder, and brown leather bracers with black feathers ring the thin arms. A dark brown hide skirt hangs from a leather belt, with a cream bone plate at the hip. The bare mauve legs end in black fur cuffs above pale mauve feet. A short staff topped with a small pale skull rises behind the other shoulder. It holds a tall scythe with a brown wooden shaft, a gray metal head and a long pale curved blade, and a dark iron ring strung with black feathers.
