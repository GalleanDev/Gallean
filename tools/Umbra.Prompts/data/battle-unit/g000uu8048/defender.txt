Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a lean mauve-skinned goblin. A heavy mane of black hair covers the back of its head. A pale gray fur tuft sits above a brown leather pauldron with tan trim. Brown leather bracers with black feathers ring the thin arms. A dark brown hide skirt hangs from a leather belt above bare mauve legs, black fur cuffs and pale mauve feet. A short staff topped with a small pale skull rises behind one shoulder. It holds a tall scythe with a brown wooden shaft and a long pale curved blade, and a dark iron ring strung with black feathers.
