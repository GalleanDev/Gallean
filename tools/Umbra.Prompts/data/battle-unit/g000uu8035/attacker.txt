Three-quarter front view of a slim air spirit with blue-white skin. Her small head is crowned with a tall fan of stiff white spikes of hair, with long pointed elf ears. Her face is cool and composed: small narrow eyes half-lidded and set in light shadow, a straight nose, a small closed mouth and a pale blue crescent painted on her cheek; her head is still. Blue curling tattoos run along her arms and legs. A fitted white bodice laced at the front and a white hip wrap cover her body, white branching sprays like frost open from her hands, and white feathery fringes flare at her ankles.
