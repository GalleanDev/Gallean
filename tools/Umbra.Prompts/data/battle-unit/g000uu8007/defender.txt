Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall figure carrying a giant purple spider on its back. The spider's bulbous dark purple body covers the whole upper back, its small rounded head at the top, and eight long jointed purple legs with dark tips spread wide around it. Below the spider a broad pale khaki cloak falls in vertical folds to the ground and hides the body, its hem edged with a row of dark pointed tips. A dark gap in the cloak shows at one side.
