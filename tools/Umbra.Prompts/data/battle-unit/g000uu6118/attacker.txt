Three-quarter front view of a slender blue-skinned woman standing beside a huge two-headed dragon. Each head rises on a long neck behind a mane of black-blue quills, with a pale banded snout and a yellow eye. The dragon's back and flanks are dark blue-gray mottled with white, its chest and belly cream with brown bands, and broad feathered wings of blue-gray rise from its shoulders. A long ringed tail curves away behind it, and its four legs end in pale claws. The woman wears a tall ornate gold headdress, a broad gold collar over her chest, gold bracers, a gold belt and a cream skirt panel over bare blue legs.
