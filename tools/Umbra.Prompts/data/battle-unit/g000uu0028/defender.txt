Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a short broad dwarf carrying a large wooden barrel on his back. The barrel is built of brown staves bound by dark iron bands studded with rivets, and it fills the whole of his back. Shaggy pale gold hair shows above it. A black chain hangs down one side of the barrel. Broad dark metal pauldrons sit on his shoulders and dark metal gauntlets with long pointed fingers cover his hands. A stained cream apron shows below the barrel, and heavy mottled brown boots cover his feet. A pale horn with a wide gold bell shows past his shoulder.
