Three-quarter front view of a short broad dwarf carrying a large wooden barrel on his back. The barrel is built of brown staves bound by dark iron bands studded with rivets. Shaggy pale gold hair and a thick beard cover his face, with round dark goggles over his eyes and a ruddy bulbous nose between them. Broad dark metal pauldrons sit on his shoulders and dark metal gauntlets with long pointed fingers cover his hands. A stained cream apron hangs from his chest to his knees under crossed brown leather straps. Heavy mottled brown boots cover his feet, and he holds a pale horn with a wide gold bell.
