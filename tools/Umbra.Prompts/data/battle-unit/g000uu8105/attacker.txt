Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged wolf under a dense shaggy coat of ragged dark gray strands tipped with pale tan. A long pale gray muzzle with an open mouth and long pale fangs juts from the mane, and a violet eye shows above it. Pointed ears rise from the mane. Smooth pale gray hide shows on the flanks and the lower legs, and the same ragged strands hang over its broad paws. The coat stands in stiff spikes along the back and shoulders.
