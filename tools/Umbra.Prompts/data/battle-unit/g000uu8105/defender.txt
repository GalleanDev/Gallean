Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged wolf under a dense shaggy coat of ragged dark gray strands tipped with pale tan. The coat stands in stiff spikes along the back and shoulders and gathers into a heavy mane over the neck and the back of the head. Smooth pale gray hide shows on the flanks and the lower legs, and the same ragged strands hang over its broad paws. The hindquarters and the sloping back are wrapped in the same tangled strands.
