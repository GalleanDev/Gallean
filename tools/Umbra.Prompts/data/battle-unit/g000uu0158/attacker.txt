Three-quarter front view of a broad dwarf in bright gold plate. Swept-back white hair tops his head under a gold band across its front, and a huge white beard spreads from under heavy white brows and a tanned weathered face over his chest to his belt. Ornate gold pauldrons and breastplate panels carry a rough embossed pattern. A wide brown leather belt with an oval steel buckle crosses his waist above a dark gray ribbed apron. Dark gray banded gauntlets cover his arms, gold thigh plates his legs, and heavy gold boots with rounded toes his feet. A cloak of brown panels bordered with ornate gold hangs from his shoulders.
