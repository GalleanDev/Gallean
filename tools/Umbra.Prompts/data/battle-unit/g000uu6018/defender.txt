Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a slender blue-skinned woman standing beside a huge two-headed dragon. One neck turns to the side and carries a pale banded snout past a mane of black-blue quills, while the other head is turned away under the same quills. The dragon's back and flanks are dark blue-gray mottled with white, and broad feathered wings of blue-gray spread from its shoulders. A long ringed tail curves away behind it, and its four legs end in pale claws. A brown leather saddle sits on its back. The woman shows a tall ornate gold headdress above a cream skirt panel and bare blue legs.
