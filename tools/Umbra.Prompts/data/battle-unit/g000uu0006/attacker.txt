Three-quarter front view of a man in banded brown leather armor. Short spiked brown hair tops his head. A thick red scarf is pulled up over his nose and mouth and wrapped around his neck, its ends hanging over his chest. Above the scarf his tanned, weathered face shows dark brows over two clearly open dark brown eyes with visible whites, looking straight ahead with a fierce, focused look. Banded pauldrons and a segmented cuirass of tan-ribbed brown leather cover his torso, and banded tassets, thigh guards and shin guards cover his legs above wide brown leather boots. A brown quiver of red-fletched arrows rides at his shoulder, and he holds a long curved bow of dark wood with a pale string.
