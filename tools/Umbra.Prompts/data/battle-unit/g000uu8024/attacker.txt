Three-quarter front view of a lean man in pale steel plate armor. A spreading crown of pale blue icy spikes rises from his head, and a teal scarf is wrapped over the lower part of his face and around his neck, leaving a pale brow bare. Segmented plates cover his shoulders, arms and legs, and a square white tasset hangs over one hip. A bright cyan cloak falls from his shoulders behind him, and he wears pale blue boots. He holds a pale blue crystalline bow with broad wing-shaped limbs and a dark navy core across his body.
