Three-quarter front view of a massive giant with gray stony skin. Dark hair with a white streak falls over his brow, and a long ragged dark beard hangs from his broad gray face. A wide yoke of pale tan overlapping scallops covers his shoulders, and a band of the same scallops circles his hips. Below it a dark brown leather kilt reaches his thighs. Thick tan wrappings bind his forearms and his ankles over dark gray blocks. His arms are heavy and blocky, ending in wide gray hands, and a row of glossy black stones lies across the knuckles of one hand. Broad bare gray feet end in blunt toes.
