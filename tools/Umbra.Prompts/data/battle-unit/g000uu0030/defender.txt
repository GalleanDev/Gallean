Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a massive giant with gray stony skin. A mass of dark ragged hair covers the back of his head. A wide yoke of pale tan overlapping scallops covers his shoulders, and a band of the same scallops circles his hips. A broad tan strip runs down his spine between them. Below it a dark brown leather kilt reaches his thighs, held by a brown belt with a metal plate. Thick tan wrappings bind his forearms and his ankles over dark gray blocks. His arms are heavy and blocky, ending in wide gray hands. Broad bare gray feet show below the wrappings.
