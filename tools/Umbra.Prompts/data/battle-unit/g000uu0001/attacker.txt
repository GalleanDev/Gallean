Three-quarter front view of a young man in brown leather armor. Short tousled blond hair, cropped close at the sides and sticking up in sharp little spikes on top, frames his fair, clean-shaven face, noble and serious like a young knight in a portrait: fine straight features, a firm chin, lips pressed together. Throughout, he keeps his eyes on his opponent to his right; his head and gaze stay turned away from the viewer, and the eyes point right. A white puffed sleeve shows at the shoulder below rounded pauldrons of dark brown leather banded with steel. A white tabard crosses his chest over a dark brown leather jerkin and hangs over a brown leather belt with a gold buckle. Dark gray sleeves and gloves cover his arms, and dark gray trousers meet tall brown leather boots with turned-down cuffs. He holds a long straight steel sword with a gold crossguard, and a red scabbard with a gold tip hangs at his hip.
