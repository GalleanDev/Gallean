Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a large four-legged dragon with a deep blue scaled hide. Its long neck rises from the shoulders in ridged blue bands, and the back of its head carries long pale ivory horns and a crest of blue fin-like spines. Large deep blue leathery wings sweep out from its back, ribbed with darker veins and folded along their trailing edges. It stands on four thick legs ending in pale ivory claws. A long tapering tail trails behind it with a row of pale ivory spines along its upper edge.
