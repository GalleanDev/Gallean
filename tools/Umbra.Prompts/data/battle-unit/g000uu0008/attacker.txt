Three-quarter front view of a young man in a long black robe. Wavy golden-blond hair, parted in the middle, falls evenly to his jaw on both sides of a fair, calm face with even features, a straight nose and quiet eyes looking down at his hands, the face of a patient scholar. The robe hangs open over a purple tunic with a gold hem band, and a dark gray mantle covers his shoulders. A brown ribbed leather pauldron sits low on one shoulder above purple puffed sleeves. His forearms cross at his chest, ending in brown gloves. White leggings meet dark brown boots.
