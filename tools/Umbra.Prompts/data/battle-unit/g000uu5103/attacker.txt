Three-quarter front view of a man in banded bronze plate. A shaggy mane of pale gray hair spills over the head under a bronze helmet band. Overlapping bronze lames cross the chest and belly over a dark gray underlayer, and a broad brown leather belt cinches the waist above layered tassets at the hips. A segmented bronze vambrace covers the forearm above a brown leather glove. A ribbed brown legging meets a heavy brown boot at the ankle. He holds a long spear with a gold-banded shaft and a pale steel head on a crossbar.
