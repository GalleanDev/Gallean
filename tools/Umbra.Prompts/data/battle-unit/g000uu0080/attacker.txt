Three-quarter front view of a lean man in dark red and gold. His head is bald and tipped forward, seen from above, so the bare pale crown fills most of it; below the crown the face turns down into shadow, where no mouth, chin or jaw line is visible, only a dark gap above the collar. The head is still. A dark red hood banded with gold wraps his neck and shoulders, and a folded pale gray cloth rises above the head. He wears a dark red tunic with wide gold bands over a black robe, with a red patterned sash at the waist and a gold apron panel hanging over the thighs. His arms and legs are wrapped in pale gray and tan bands, and gold anklets sit above wrapped bare feet. A thin pale strip hangs from the belt.
