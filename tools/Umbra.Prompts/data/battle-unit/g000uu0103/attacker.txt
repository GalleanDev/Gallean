Three-quarter front view of a robed rider on a black horse with bat wings. The horse has a dark gray-black hide, a long neck, cloven black hooves and a pale gray skull-like head in profile with a long muzzle and a dark socket. Its wings spread wide to either side, and their membranes are dark red. The rider sits high on its back, wrapped in bright purple ribbed robes under a black mantle. Its head is a ridged yellow-brown skull, and two large gray clawed hands rest in front of the robes. Two long curved horns rise beside the horse's head.
