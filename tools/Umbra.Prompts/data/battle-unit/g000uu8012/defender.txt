Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of an old man in a long pale gray robe. A gray hood covers the back of his head, and pale pink pointed ears jut out. A gray mantle edged with brown fur hangs from his shoulders down the back of the robe, which falls in long vertical folds to a dark brown and gold hem band above a broad flat pale slab. Wide sleeves with brown fur cuffs cover his arms. A long brown wooden shaft rises to a staff wrapped in gold at its head, under a spray of golden-brown leaves. Gnarled bare brown branches rise behind him.
