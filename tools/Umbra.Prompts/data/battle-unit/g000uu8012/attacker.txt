Three-quarter front view of an old man in a long pale gray robe. A gray hood frames a lined face with a long dark beard, and pale pink pointed ears jut out. A gray mantle edged with brown fur covers his shoulders above wide sleeves with brown fur cuffs. A pale tan panel crossed by brown straps covers his chest below a small amber clasp. A green embossed panel runs down the robe to a dark brown and gold hem band above a broad flat pale slab. He holds a staff wrapped in gold at its head under a spray of golden-brown leaves. Gnarled bare brown branches rise behind him.
