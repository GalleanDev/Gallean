Three-quarter front view of a robed man with a tall pointed black hood standing over him. Beneath the hood sits a pale skull-like head with dark hollows, above a red collar band. Enormous rounded pauldrons of pale banded plate cover both shoulders. A black robe falls from them to the ground, opening on bright red inner panels down its length, and a red sash with a small buckle crosses the waist. Wide black sleeves end in bare dark hands held out at his sides.
