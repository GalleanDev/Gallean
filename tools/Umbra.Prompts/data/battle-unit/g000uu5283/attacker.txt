Three-quarter front view of an armored man in black plate. A rounded black helm covers the head, and a folded white cloth cravat hangs at the throat over the breastplate. A tall standing collar with gold edging and red lining rises behind the head. Black pauldrons and a black cuirass are traced with fine gold lines, and dark gauntlets cover the hands. A deep red cape falls from the shoulders to the ground on both sides. Black leg plates are inlaid with pale bone-colored segments above pale armored boots.
