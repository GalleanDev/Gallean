Three-quarter front view of a woman in a long red cloak. A smooth pale gray cloth hood covers the top of her head and turns red lower down where a red patterned headband crosses her brow. Below it is the small face of a grave woman of thirty-five: smooth tan skin, a straight thin nose, natural pale lips without any lipstick, the mouth closed and still. White cloth panels hang close on either side of it. Broad brown pauldrons with gold tracery sit on her shoulders over pale gray sleeves. Silver plates cross her chest, and a white panel patterned in red runs down the cloak to a silver hem above steel toe caps.
