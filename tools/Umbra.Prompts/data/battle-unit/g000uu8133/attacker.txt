Three-quarter front view of a slender elf woman bending forward in a long brown robe. Her head is crowned with gray thorny twigs and small red berries, and dark hair is drawn back beneath it; from the side of her head a long, narrow, pointed elven ear sticks out backward past the twigs, tan like her skin. A thick rolled collar of dark red cloth wraps her neck above a dark red cloak. One bare hand shows at her sleeve. Below the waist the robe widens into a hem of gray bark hung with red and orange leaves that reaches the ground.
