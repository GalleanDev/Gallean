Three-quarter front view of a man in heavy layered robes. A dark spiked headpiece sits low over his pale gaunt face, his ears are pointed, and a deep red hood rises behind his head. Rounded steel pauldrons with a tan-olive rim cap his shoulders, above a white cloth knot at his throat. A long robe striped in dark brown, olive and red covers him to the ground, with a belt of pale beads at the waist and gray steel plates over the thighs. A deep red cloak hangs open at his sides. He holds a long dark staff topped with a pale forked head.
