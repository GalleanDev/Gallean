Three-quarter front view of a slim young man in a long green cloak, bowing his head so the wide brown brim of his green hat hides his brow. The dark brown below the brim is his straight dark hair, falling flat over his temples and ears to his jaw. His face is lean and plain, olive-brown, with a narrow nose, small eyes looking down and a calm closed mouth. A white robe falls between the cloak folds to his feet, which wear heavy dark brown leather boots with hard flat soles, square toes and polished steel toe caps; the boots are smooth leather, with no toes, no claws and no fur.
