Three-quarter front view of a skeleton in a deep red robe. Its bare bone skull shows dark empty sockets above a black collar. A black iron wheel of spokes hung with pale bones rises behind the shoulders. Black plates cover the shoulders, and pale arm bones and pale leg bones show bare between the cloth. The deep red robe hangs from the chest to the ankles in heavy folds in front of a long black cloak, and bony feet stand below it. It holds a gray sword with a broad flat blade.
