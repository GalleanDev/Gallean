Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a hunched winged beast with mottled brown-bronze stone hide. Two large membranous wings open wide from its shoulders, carried on long thin finger spars, their outer edges falling in deep scallops. Rough chipped plates ridge its back and short spikes stand along them. The ridged back of its horned head sits low between the wing shoulders. A long thick tail curves down from its hips and tapers to a point, and its thick limbs end in broad clawed feet.
