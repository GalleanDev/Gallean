Three-quarter front view of a hunched winged beast with mottled brown-bronze stone hide. Curved horns sweep back from its head above a blunt snout, and its yellow eyes sit deep under a heavy ridge. Rough chipped plates ridge its shoulders and back, and short spikes stand along them. Two large membranous wings open from its shoulders, one raised high and one sweeping low, each carried on long thin finger spars. It crouches forward on thick limbs that end in broad clawed feet, and heavy claws tip its hands.
