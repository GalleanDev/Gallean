Three-quarter front view of a man wrapped from head to foot in a deep red hooded cloak. Pale gray braided trim edges the hood and runs down across the chest. The hood is drawn up over his head and its opening is a dark hollow. The heavy cloak hangs in long vertical folds to the ground and hides the body, and a patch of studded brown leather shows low down where the folds part. A slender straight dagger with a pale steel blade juts out from the cloak at waist height.
