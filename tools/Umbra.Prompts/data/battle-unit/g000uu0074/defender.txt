Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in a deep red hooded cloak. The hood is drawn up over the back of his head and edged with pale gray braided trim. The cloak is swept off the shoulders and falls down one side in long folds to the ground. Studded brown leather covers his back and arms in overlapping plates and straps, and brown leather boots cover his feet. A slender straight dagger with a pale steel blade juts out at waist height, and a second one juts out beside it.
