Three-quarter front view of an enormously fat squat creature whose head sits on top of its bulging body with no neck. Its hide is warm brown and leathery, creased into deep folds. Heavy wrinkled brows hang over two narrow slit eyes, and a long red tongue lolls from a wide slit mouth. Pointed ears stick out at each side of the broad crown. Short thick arms end in wide hands of fat blunt fingers with pale nails, and the round belly sags to stubby feet with fat toes.
