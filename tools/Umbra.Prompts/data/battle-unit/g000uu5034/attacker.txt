Three-quarter front view of a small thin imp with reddish-brown wrinkled skin. Its face is the lower half of its wide, flat head, below a heavy wrinkled brow: a hideous demon face, eyes small and dark and pushed out to the left and right ends of the brow, no round rims around them, a wide grimacing mouth with a small red tongue that sticks out and draws back in as it moves. Large pointed ears spread out to either side. Its bare body is lean and knobbly, with a paler tan chest and belly. Thin arms end in long bony fingers, and its thin legs bend at knobby knees above broad flat feet with pale toes. A long thin tail trails on the ground behind it.
