Three-quarter front view of a pale-skinned rider in bright green ribbed armor on a heavy horned mount. He wears a high black collar and a black mantle, and he holds a long dark blade with a hooked guard. His mouth is a thin pale line, colorless against his gray skin and almost invisible, sealed shut and unmoving from the first frame to the last, and his face holds one fixed expression throughout. The mount's head is cased in green and black armor with a long snout and thin copper spikes rising from its crown, and dark brown straps cross its neck and shoulders. A long shaggy black coat hangs from its back to the ground, ending in a pale gold hem band above black hooves.
