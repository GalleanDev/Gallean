Three-quarter front view of a tall figure in a bell-shaped robe. A broad pale bone crest with two long curved prongs spreads above a dark teal hood that hangs over the head, and under the hood shows the face of an undead elf: gaunt, gray, rotting skin, sunken glowing orange eyes, a thin nose and thin lips, with no gaping maw. Dark teal cloth wraps the shoulders over pale bone plates bound with tan straps, and a dark red patch sits at the waist. Gray armor plates cap the hips above a wide skirt of black and deep red set with a row of large round gray discs and edged with a pale rim at the floor. A curved horn is fixed at one hip, its wide gray bell turned toward the viewer.
