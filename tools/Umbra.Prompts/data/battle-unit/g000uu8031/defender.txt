Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a woman in a floor-length gold gown with a brocade pattern. A long fall of pale blonde hair covers the back of her head and hangs the whole length of her back. Cream sleeves with gold cuffs run down her arms to pale bare hands, and gold bands sit at her shoulders. The gown draws in at the waist and opens below it into a full skirt that sweeps the ground in heavy folds, the gold cloth worked with darker swirling patterns.
