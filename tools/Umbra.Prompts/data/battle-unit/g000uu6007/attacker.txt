Three-quarter front view of a tall gray-skinned elf with a giant purple spider behind him. Long white hair falls over most of his lowered gray face, leaving only a sunken cheek and a pointed ear in view. Eight long jointed purple legs with dark tips spread around him, and a cluster of small magenta eyes sits on the spider's bulbous head above him. He wears a dark purple robe with a long purple tabard bearing a pale swirling pattern, pale khaki drapes over the shoulders, and a broad khaki cloak behind him. A dark belt with a gold buckle crosses his waist. Dark segmented plates cover his arms and shins above gray armored boots, and his gray fingers are bare.
