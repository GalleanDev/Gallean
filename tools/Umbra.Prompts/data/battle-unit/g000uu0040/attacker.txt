Three-quarter front view of a stout dwarf in dark brown studded armor. A white topknot bound with an orange band rises from his head above a brown headband, and a long white beard bound with orange bands falls down his chest. Dark brown shoulder guards carry long curved tan horns, and a horned beast skull sits on one shoulder. A broad brown belt with studs crosses his waist above a red and white striped kilt, and gray steel boots cover his feet.
