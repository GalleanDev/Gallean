Three-quarter front view of a centaur with a pale gray human torso and a near-black horse body. Long black hair hangs in matted strands around his face, where a pointed ear and a dark beard show. His chest and arms are bare and muscled, and a dark cuff wraps one wrist. A ragged pale gray pelt is draped over his shoulder and hangs down the horse's side, and a rust-orange wrap crosses his waist and hangs down in a long strip. A rust-orange band circles the horse's barrel. He holds a long pale shaft with a broad wavy gray-blue blade. Shaggy black fetlocks hang over his dark hooves.
