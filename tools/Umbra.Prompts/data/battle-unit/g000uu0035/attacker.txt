Three-quarter front view of a short woman in a long white robe, her head bowed and wound in layers of white cloth. The wrap covers her head, neck and nearly her whole face; no face is visible, only a small shadowed hollow in the folds where her face would be. Above the wrap a crown of stiff golden straw-like spikes fans upward. A red mantle with a gold trim band falls over a pale gray breastplate, a tan belt sits above a red sash, and the robe hangs to pale boots. A dark wooden staff with a squared head rises behind her shoulder.
