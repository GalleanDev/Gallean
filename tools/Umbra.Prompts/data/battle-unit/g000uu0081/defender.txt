Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a man in a long dark green robe. A green skullcap covers the back of his head, and two long ribbed pale horns curve out from it to either side. A broad brass mantle worked with raised patterns lies across his shoulders, and a large brown segmented shoulder guard edged with gold sits on one of them. A second brass band crosses the waist, and the robe falls in long folds to a brass hem at the ground. His arms are brown and segmented and end in dark clawed hands.
