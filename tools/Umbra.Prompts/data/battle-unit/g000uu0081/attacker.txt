Three-quarter front view of a man in a long dark green robe. A green skullcap with a round gold boss sits on his head, and two long ribbed pale horns curve out from it to either side. A dark green cloth scarf covers his mouth and chin, and above it only the upper half of his face shows: the bridge of his nose and two shadowed eyes under the skullcap. A broad brass mantle lies across his shoulders and chest, with an oval gold medallion at its center and a pale gray tassel under it. A brown segmented shoulder guard sits on one shoulder, and the robe falls to a brass hem above brown boots. His arms are brown and segmented and end in dark clawed hands.
