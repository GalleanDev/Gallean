Three-quarter front view of a gaunt man in a tall pale cream cylindrical hat, open at its top. His face is covered by a plain white mask, smooth and blank, with only two dark holes for the eyes, between pointed ears. A high black collar sits above a close white sleeveless bodice seamed in dark thread, and his thin gray arms are bare to brass cuffs at the wrists. A red panel hangs at the front of his waist over a black apron and dark trousers, and cream bands wrap his shins above black boots. He carries two broad dark cleavers with pale steel edges.
