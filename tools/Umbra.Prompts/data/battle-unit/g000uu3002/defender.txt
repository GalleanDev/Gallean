Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a squat broad giant in heavy dark blue plate armor. The back of a rounded black helmet with a short upright spike sits low between the shoulders. A tan-gold fringed mantle covers the shoulders and upper back over patches of coarse olive fur. The plates across the back are engraved with deep swirling lines above a wide banded skirt and a studded belt. Curved dark spikes rise from the pauldrons and the arms hang in clawed gauntlets. He holds a huge maul with a blocky dark stone head on a pale wooden haft. Squat legs end in broad armored boots.
