Three-quarter front view of a squat broad giant in heavy dark blue plate armor. A rounded black helmet with a short upright spike and a curl of gold scrollwork covers the head. A tan-gold fringed mantle is draped across the shoulders and chest over patches of coarse olive fur. The plates are engraved with deep swirling lines and swell into a heavy rounded belly above a wide banded skirt and a studded belt. Curved dark spikes rise from the pauldrons and the forearms end in clawed gauntlets. He holds a huge maul with a blocky dark stone head on a pale wooden haft. Squat legs end in broad armored boots.
