Three-quarter front view of a lean elf in dark leather armor. A bright green spiky crest rises over dark hair, and pale pointed ears stand out beside a pale face marked with dark stripes. Segmented dark brown leather pauldrons sit above sleeves and bracers of the same leather. A dark green quilted tunic opens at the chest over a pale ribbed undershirt, and a wide brown leather belt with a gold stud carries a brown leather pouch at the hip. Dark brown leather leggings and boots cover his legs. He holds a long tan bow with curled tips.
