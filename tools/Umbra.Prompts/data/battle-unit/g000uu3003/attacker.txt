Three-quarter front view of a tall horned demon with a hide of dark blue armored plates veined in gold. Pale bone spines jut from its shoulders, arms, chest and thighs, and a crown of taller spines rises from its head. Small yellow eyes sit in its dark face. Two narrow blade-like wings, dark blue and ribbed with gold, spread from its back. Thick muscled arms end in long pale claws, and its digitigrade legs end in broad clawed feet.
