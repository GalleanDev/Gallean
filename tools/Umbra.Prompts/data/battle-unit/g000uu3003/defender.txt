Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall horned demon with a hide of dark blue armored plates veined in gold. Pale bone spines jut from its shoulders, arms, back and thighs, and a crown of taller spines rises from the back of its head. Two narrow blade-like wings, dark blue and ribbed with gold, spread from its shoulder blades. A long segmented tail lined with pale spines trails to the ground. Thick muscled arms end in long pale claws, and its digitigrade legs end in broad clawed feet.
