Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a tall figure in a long black hooded robe. The hood is drawn up over the back of its head, and a gold band crosses the shoulders below it. The robe falls in long heavy folds down its back and spreads at the ground, where a gold-trimmed hem shows. Its pale forearms are wrapped in black and white bands, and its long pale fingers reach past the sleeves at either side.
