Three-quarter front view of two skeletal torsos rising from a heap of dark red sinew. Each bare white skull has hollow sockets with small red eyes and is capped by a dark blue-black hood trimmed with chain. Red cords wrap the necks, spines and bony arms, the ribs stand open across each chest, and dark blue-black plates studded with gold cover the shoulders and forearms above skeletal hands. Below them the heap falls in ribbed strands of dark red flesh, and a large face and two smaller ones are pressed into its surface. Thin red tendrils trail out along the ground.
