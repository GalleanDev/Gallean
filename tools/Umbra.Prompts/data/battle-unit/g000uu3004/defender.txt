Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of two skeletal torsos rising from a heap of dark red sinew. Each head is a dark blue-black hood trimmed with chain. Red cords wrap the necks, spines and bony arms, the ribs stand open across each back, and dark blue-black plates studded with gold cover the shoulders and forearms above skeletal hands. Below them the heap falls in ribbed strands of dark red flesh with dim head shapes pressed into its surface. Thin red tendrils trail out along the ground.
