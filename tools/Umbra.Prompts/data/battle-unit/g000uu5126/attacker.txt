Three-quarter front view of a mermaid seated among a tangle of dark brown twisting roots. Her skin is pale gray-white on the chest and arms and dark teal over the shoulders. Long dark teal hair falls past her shoulders beneath a tall red crest, and pale webbed fins flank her head. Her pale gray-green face has gray eyes and orange-red lips. Ridged gold plates cap both shoulders above a dark teal scaled bodice below her bare chest. Below the waist a long teal fish tail edged in pale pink opens into a broad fan. She holds a tall spear with a ridged dark red shaft and a pale steel point.
