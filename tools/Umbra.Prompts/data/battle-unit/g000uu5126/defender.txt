Rear three-quarter view, turned away from the viewer, of a mermaid seated among a tangle of dark brown twisting roots. Long dark teal hair covers the back of her head beneath a tall red crest, with pale webbed fins to either side. Her skin is pale gray-white on the arms and dark teal over the shoulders and down the back. Ridged gold plates cap both shoulders above a dark teal scaled bodice. Below the waist she has a long teal fish tail edged in pale pink that opens into a broad fan. She holds a tall spear with a ridged dark red shaft and a pale steel point.
