Three-quarter front view of a large four-legged dragon with a deep blue scaled hide. Lighter gray-blue bands run down its throat, chest and belly. Its long neck lifts a narrow head crowned with long pale ivory horns and a crest of blue fin-like spines, and an amber eye sits above a long snout with pale teeth. Large deep blue leathery wings rise from its shoulders, one spread wide and ribbed with darker veins. It stands on four thick legs ending in pale ivory claws, and a long tapering tail curves behind it with a row of pale ivory spines along its upper edge.
