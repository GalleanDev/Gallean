# Packer

Packer builds a `.galleanpack` from your own copy of the original game and can enhance it with the 4K asset pipeline.

## Enhance

The `enhance` command runs (with `--device=gpu`) the enhancement locally. Enhancing a pack needs a GPU with at least 12 GB of VRAM and 24 GB of RAM, and 16 GB of VRAM with 32 GB of RAM are recommended.
